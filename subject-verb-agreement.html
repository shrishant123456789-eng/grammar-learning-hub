Introduction

Subject–Verb Agreement means that the subject and the verb must match each other in number (singular or plural).

The Golden Rule:
Singular Subject → Singular Verb (is, was, has, plays)
Plural Subject → Plural Verb (are, were, have, play)
📌 Hinglish: Agar Subject ek hai (Singular), toh Verb bhi singular hoga. Agar Subject ek se zyada hai (Plural), toh Verb bhi plural hoga.
