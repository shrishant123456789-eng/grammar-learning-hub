The Final Polish

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

Bahut baar humein lagta hai hum sahi English bol rahe hain, lekin hum wahi purani galtiyan repeat karte hain (jaise "I am agree"). Ye choti galtiyan aapka confidence kam karti hain.

Polishing ka matlab hai: In choti galtiyon ko hatana, pronunciation ko saaf karna, aur English ko ek 'Subject' nahi, ek 'Habit' banana.

🇬🇧 Mastery Principles

  • Self-Correction: Catch your own mistakes before others do.
  • No MTI: Reduce Mother Tongue Influence for global clarity.
  • Habit: Don't translate. React directly in English.
