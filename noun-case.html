Noun: Case

Clear Rules + Examples + Practice (Basic to Advanced)

📘 What is Case?

The case of a noun shows the relation of the noun to other words in a sentence.

📗 Kinds of Noun Case

1. Nominative Case
A noun in the nominative case is the subject of a verb.
Ram plays cricket.
(The word Ram is the subject.)
2. Objective Case
A noun in the objective case is the object of a verb or preposition.
She saw Ram.
He sat on the chair.
3. Possessive Case
A noun in the possessive case shows ownership or possession.
Ram’s book, the boy’s bag, girls’ hostel

📙 Important Notes

  • Nominative case answers who? or what?
  • Objective case answers whom? or what?
  • Possessive case usually uses ’s or ’
