Definition

We use the Simple Past Tense for actions that started and finished at a specific time in the past.

Hinglish Explanation:
Hum Simple Past ka use tab karte hain jab koi kaam past me poora ho chuka ho aur uska time pata ho (jaise kal, pichle saal, subah). Isme kaam khatam ho chuka hota hai.

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + V2 (2nd form of verb) + Object
Negative: Subject + did not + V1 + Object
Interrogative: Did + Subject + V1 + Object?

Helping Verbs & Signal Words

Helping Verb: 'Did' (only in Negative & Interrogative sentences).

Signal Words: Yesterday, ago, last night, in 2010, last week, once upon a time.

Examples

  • I played cricket yesterday. (Basic)
  • She did not go to school last Monday. (Negative)
  • Did you watch the movie? (Interrogative)
  • They lived in London for five years. (Medium)
  • The ancient Romans built these roads. (Advanced)

Common Mistakes

❌ I did not went there.
✔ I did not go there.
👉 After 'did', always use V1 (Base Form).
❌ She sleeped late.
✔ She slept late.
👉 'Sleep' is an irregular verb. V2 is 'slept'.
❌ I have seen him yesterday.
✔ I saw him yesterday.
👉 'Yesterday' specifies time, so use Simple Past, not Present Perfect.

Mini Practice (Simple Past)

Definition

Used to describe actions that were going on (in progress) at a specific time in the past.

Hinglish Explanation:
Jab past me koi kaam chal raha tha (jaise "woh khel raha tha", "hum padh rahe the"), tab hum Past Continuous use karte hain.

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + was/were + V1 + ing + Object
Negative: Subject + was/were + not + V1 + ing...
Interrogative: Was/Were + Subject + V1 + ing...?

Helping Verbs

Was: I, He, She, It, Singular Noun.
Were: You, We, They, Plural Noun.

Examples

  • I was reading a book when you called.
  • They were playing football in the rain.
  • She was not listening to the teacher.
  • Were you sleeping at 10 PM?
  • The wind was blowing fiercely last night.

Common Mistakes

❌ They was going.
✔ They were going.
👉 'They' is plural, so use 'were'.
❌ I were happy.
✔ I was happy.
👉 'I' takes 'was' in standard grammar.
❌ She was cook food.
✔ She was cooking food.
👉 Continuous tense always needs 'ing'.

Mini Practice (Past Continuous)

Definition

Used for an action that was completed before another action took place in the past.

Hinglish Explanation:
Jab past me do kaam hue hon, to jo kaam pehle khatam hua uske liye Past Perfect (Had + V3) aur jo baad me hua uske liye Simple Past (V2) use karte hain. Eg: "Doctor ke aane se pehle mareez mar chuka tha."

Structure / Formula

Formula: Subject + had + V3 (3rd form) + Object
Key Words: already, before, after, by the time.

Examples

  • She had left before I arrived.
  • I had never seen such a beautiful car before.
  • The train had departed when we reached the station.
  • He realized that he had lost his wallet.

Common Mistakes

❌ I had went to school.
✔ I had gone to school.
👉 After 'had', always use V3 (Gone), not V2 (Went).
❌ She had ate food.
✔ She had eaten food.
👉 V3 of 'eat' is 'eaten'.

Mini Practice (Past Perfect)

Definition

Used to show that an action started in the past and continued up until another time in the past.

Hinglish Explanation:
Jab koi kaam past me shuru hua aur past me hi kisi khaas samay tak chalta raha. Isme 'time' (since/for) ka use hota hai. Eg: "Woh 2 ghante se padh raha tha."

Structure / Formula

Formula: Subject + had been + V1 + ing + Object + (Since/For + Time)

Since vs For

Since: Point of time (2 PM, Monday, 1990).
For: Duration of time (2 hours, 5 years, a long time).

Examples

  • He had been waiting for two hours when she finally arrived.
  • They had been playing cricket since morning.
  • She had been working at that company for 10 years before she quit.

Common Mistakes

❌ He was playing for 2 hours.
✔ He had been playing for 2 hours.
👉 If duration is given, avoid Past Continuous; use Past Perfect Continuous.
