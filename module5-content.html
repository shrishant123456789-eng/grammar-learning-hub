1️⃣ He / She + Verb "s" Mistake

"Ye sabse common galti hai! Hindi me hum bolte hain 'Woh khaata hai', par English me 'He eat' galat hai. Humein verb ke saath 's' ya 'es' lagana padta hai."

The Golden Rule

If the subject is He, She, It, or a Name (Singular), you MUST add 's' or 'es' to the verb.

❌ He go to market. (Wrong)
✅ He goes to market. (Correct)

❌ She play cricket. (Wrong)
✅ She plays cricket. (Correct)

Why do we forget this?

Because 'I', 'You', 'We', and 'They' do NOT take 's'. We get confused when switching between "I go" and "He goes".

2️⃣ Do vs Does Confusion

"Sawaal poochte waqt 'Do' lagayein ya 'Does'? Iska simple rule yaad kar lo."
  • DO: I, You, We, They (Plural)
  • DOES: He, She, It, Ravi (Singular)
SubjectQuestion (Wrong)Question (Correct)
YouDoes you like tea?Do you like tea?
SheDo she know you?Does she know you?
TheyDoes they play?Do they play?

3️⃣ Is / Am / Are Confusion

Use these only for "Continuous Actions" (ING) or "States" (Adjectives/Nouns). Do not use them with action verbs in Simple Present.

Common Mistake: "I am agree with you." ❌
Correction: "I agree with you." ✅
(Agree is a verb, so no need for 'am')

Correct Usage:

  • I am: Happy, Working, A teacher.
  • He/She is: Sad, Running, My friend.
  • We/They are: Late, Eating, Students.

4️⃣ Wrong Word Order (Hindi → English)

"Hindi me hum bolte hain: Main (Subject) Market (Object) Jaa raha hoon (Verb).
English me order change ho jata hai: Subject + Verb + Object."
StructureExample
Hindi Style (Wrong)I market go.
English Style (Correct)I go to market.

More Examples:

  • ❌ "Why you are crying?" (Statement order in question)
  • ✅ "Why are you crying?" (Verb comes before subject in WH questions)

5️⃣ ❌➡️✅ Daily Life Wrong vs Correct

He/She + S Mistakes

❌ Wrong✅ CorrectReason
He like pizza.He likes pizza.Third person singular takes 's'.
My father work in a bank.My father works in a bank.Father = He (Singular).
She don't know.She doesn't know.She takes 'Does', not 'Do'.

General Spoken Errors

❌ Wrong✅ CorrectReason
I am having a car.I have a car.Possession uses Simple Present.
She is my cousin sister.She is my cousin.'Cousin' includes gender/relation context.
I didn't went there.I didn't go there.Did takes 1st form of verb.
Please return back my money.Please return my money.Return means 'give back'.

6️⃣ Spoken Sentence Patterns

Memorize these frames to avoid grammar mistakes automatically.

Pattern 1: Did you...?
Use only V1 (First form of verb).
👉 Did you eat? (Not Ate)
👉 Did you call? (Not Called)
Pattern 2: Does he/she...?
Use only V1 (Remove 's' in question).
👉 Does he play? (Not Plays)
👉 Does she know? (Not Knows)

7️⃣ Common Indian English Errors

  • Discussion: ❌ "Discuss about politics" → ✅ "Discuss politics".
  • Marriage: ❌ "He is married with her" → ✅ "He is married to her".
  • Years: ❌ "I have 20 years." → ✅ "I am 20 years old".
  • Reply: ❌ "Reply me back." → ✅ "Reply to me".
  • Exam: ❌ "I gave an exam." → ✅ "I took an exam." (Student takes, Teacher gives).

8️⃣ Confidence & Fluency Tips

Why mistakes are good?

Mistakes show you are trying. Native speakers also make mistakes. The goal is communication, not perfection.

The 10-Minute Rule

"Roz 10 minute mirror ke saamne khade hokar zor se English bolein. Agar galti ho, toh wahin rukein aur correct sentence wapas bolein."
  • Don't translate word-to-word.
  • Think in whole sentences.
  • Record your voice and listen.

9️⃣ Speak Yourself (Correction Drill)

Read the WRONG sentence aloud, then immediately say the CORRECT sentence loudly.

Wrong: "Yesterday I meet him."
⬇️
Say Loudly: "Yesterday I MET him."
Wrong: "He don't have money."
⬇️
Say Loudly: "He DOESN'T have money."
Wrong: "I am going to home."
⬇️
Say Loudly: "I am going HOME." (No 'to' before home)
