Noun: Gender

Clear Rules + Examples + Practice (Basic to Advanced)

📘 What is Gender?

Gender of a noun shows whether the noun refers to a male, female, or neither.

📗 Kinds of Gender

1. Masculine Gender
A noun that denotes a male.
boy, man, king, father
2. Feminine Gender
A noun that denotes a female.
girl, woman, queen, mother
3. Common Gender
A noun that denotes both male and female.
child, teacher, student, doctor
4. Neuter Gender
A noun that denotes neither male nor female.
book, table, car, pen

📙 Important Notes

  • Masculine ↔ Feminine may change by different words (king–queen)
  • Some nouns remain the same for both genders (teacher, friend)
  • Neuter gender is used for lifeless things
