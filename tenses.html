▶ Present Tense

▶ Present Indefinite Tense

Definition: Present Indefinite Tense is used to express daily habits, general facts, universal truths and permanent situations.

Formula:
Affirmative: Subject + V1 / V1(s, es)
Negative: Subject + do/does + not + V1
Interrogative: Do/Does + Subject + V1?

Examples:
• He plays cricket.
• The sun rises in the east.
• We respect our teachers.
1. She ___ to school every day. (go)
2. They ___ football on Sunday. (play)
3. He ___ not like coffee.
4. ___ you understand English?

▶ Present Continuous Tense

Definition: Used to show an action that is happening at the time of speaking.

Formula:
Affirmative: Subject + is/am/are + V-ing
Negative: Subject + is/am/are + not + V-ing
Interrogative: Is/Am/Are + Subject + V-ing?

Examples:
• She is reading a book.
• They are playing cricket.
1. I ___ reading a book now.

▶ Present Perfect Tense

Definition: Used for an action completed in the past but its effect is seen in the present.

Formula:
Affirmative: Subject + has/have + V3
Negative: Subject + has/have + not + V3
Interrogative: Has/Have + Subject + V3?

Examples:
• I have finished my work.
• She has gone home.
