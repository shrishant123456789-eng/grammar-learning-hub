Simple Present

Used for habits, general truths, and permanent situations

Present Continuous

Used for actions happening now or around now

Present Perfect

Used for past actions with present relevance

Present Perfect Continuous

Used for ongoing actions that started in the past

Definition

Simple Present tense is used to express habitual actions, general truths, scientific facts, and permanent situations.

Hinglish Explanation

सिंपल प्रेजेंट किसी भी आदत या सच्चाई को बताने के लिए use होता है। जैसे - मैं रोज़ सुबह 7 बजे उठता हूं।

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + Base Verb (+s/es for he/she/it)
Negative: Subject + do/does + not + Base Verb
Interrogative: Do/Does + Subject + Base Verb?

Helping Verbs

Do, Does (for questions and negatives)

Signal Words

always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, regularly, daily

Examples

  • I eat breakfast at 8 AM every day.
  • She goes to school by bus.
  • They play cricket on Sundays.
  • The sun rises in the east.
  • Water boils at 100°C.
  • He works in a bank.
  • We live in Mumbai.
  • Birds fly in the sky.

Common Mistakes

Wrong: He go to school daily.

Right: He goes to school daily.

Why: With he/she/it, add 's' or 'es' to the verb.

Definition

Present Continuous tense is used for actions happening at the moment of speaking or temporary situations.

Hinglish Explanation

प्रेजेंट कंटीन्यूउस उन कामों के लिए use होता है जो अभी हो रहे हैं। जैसे - मैं अभी खाना खा रहा हूं।

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + am/is/are + Verb+ing
Negative: Subject + am/is/are + not + Verb+ing
Interrogative: Am/Is/Are + Subject + Verb+ing?

Helping Verbs

am, is, are

Signal Words

now, at the moment, currently, right now, these days

Examples

  • I am studying English now.
  • She is cooking dinner.
  • They are playing football.
  • We are watching TV.
  • He is reading a book.
  • The children are sleeping.
  • It is raining outside.
  • You are learning grammar.

Common Mistakes

Wrong: I am go to school now.

Right: I am going to school now.

Why: After am/is/are, use verb+ing form.

Definition

Present Perfect tense is used for actions that happened at an unspecified time in the past or actions that started in the past and continue to the present.

Hinglish Explanation

प्रेजेंट परफेक्ट उन कामों के लिए use होता है जो अनिश्चित समय पर हुए हैं या past से शुरू होकर अभी भी चल रहे हैं।

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + have/has + Past Participle
Negative: Subject + have/has + not + Past Participle
Interrogative: Have/Has + Subject + Past Participle?

Helping Verbs

have, has

Signal Words

already, just, yet, ever, never, recently, lately, since, for

Examples

  • I have finished my homework.
  • She has visited Paris.
  • They have lived here for ten years.
  • We have just arrived.
  • He has already eaten.
  • I have never seen that movie.
  • The train has left.
  • She has worked here since 2020.

Common Mistakes

Wrong: I have seen him yesterday.

Right: I saw him yesterday.

Why: With specific past time (yesterday), use Simple Past, not Present Perfect.

Definition

Present Perfect Continuous tense is used for actions that started in the past and are still continuing or have recently stopped.

Hinglish Explanation

प्रेजेंट परफेक्ट कंटीन्यूउस उन कामों के लिए use होता है जो past से शुरू होकर अभी भी चल रहे हैं।

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + have/has + been + Verb+ing
Negative: Subject + have/has + not + been + Verb+ing
Interrogative: Have/Has + Subject + been + Verb+ing?

Helping Verbs

have been, has been

Signal Words

for, since, how long, all day, recently

Examples

  • I have been studying for two hours.
  • She has been working here since 2020.
  • They have been playing cricket all day.
  • We have been waiting for you.
  • He has been reading that book.
  • It has been raining since morning.
  • You have been learning English for months.
  • The children have been sleeping.

Common Mistakes

Wrong: I have been knowing him for years.

Right: I have known him for years.

Why: 'Know' is a stative verb - doesn't use continuous form.

Simple Past

Used for completed actions in the past

Past Continuous

Used for ongoing actions in the past

Past Perfect

Used for actions completed before another past action

Past Perfect Continuous

Used for ongoing actions before another past action

Definition

Simple Past tense is used to express actions that were completed at a specific time in the past.

Hinglish Explanation

सिंपल पास्ट उन कामों के लिए use होता है जो past में किसी खास समय पर पूरे हुए।

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + Past Verb
Negative: Subject + did + not + Base Verb
Interrogative: Did + Subject + Base Verb?

Helping Verbs

did (for questions and negatives)

Signal Words

yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020, then, when

Examples

  • I visited my grandmother yesterday.
  • She studied English last year.
  • They played football in the morning.
  • We watched a movie last night.
  • He worked hard for the exam.
  • I bought a new car.
  • She wrote a letter to her friend.
  • The train arrived on time.

Common Mistakes

Wrong: I didn't went to school.

Right: I didn't go to school.

Why: After 'did', always use base form of verb.

Definition

Past Continuous tense is used for actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past.

Hinglish Explanation

पास्ट कंटीन्यूउस उन कामों के लिए use होता है जो past में किसी समय चल रहे थे।

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + was/were + Verb+ing
Negative: Subject + was/were + not + Verb+ing
Interrogative: Was/Were + Subject + Verb+ing?

Helping Verbs

was, were

Signal Words

while, when, as, at that time, at 8 PM yesterday

Examples

  • I was studying at 8 PM yesterday.
  • She was cooking when I arrived.
  • They were playing cricket all evening.
  • We were watching TV at that time.
  • He was reading a book while waiting.
  • It was raining heavily.
  • The children were sleeping peacefully.
  • You were talking on the phone.

Common Mistakes

Wrong: They was playing football.

Right: They were playing football.

Why: Use 'were' with plural subjects (they/we/you).

Definition

Past Perfect tense is used for actions that were completed before another action in the past.

Hinglish Explanation

पास्ट परफेक्ट उन कामों के लिए use होता है जो past में किसी दूसरे काम से पहले पूरे हुए।

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + had + Past Participle
Negative: Subject + had + not + Past Participle
Interrogative: Had + Subject + Past Participle?

Helping Verbs

had

Signal Words

already, before, after, by the time, when, until

Examples

  • I had finished my homework before dinner.
  • She had left when I arrived.
  • They had completed the project by Friday.
  • We had never seen that movie before.
  • He had already eaten when we called him.
  • The train had left before we reached.
  • I had studied English before moving to USA.
  • She had written three books by 2020.

Common Mistakes

Wrong: I had went to school before you came.

Right: I had gone to school before you came.

Why: After 'had', use past participle form (gone, not went).

Definition

Past Perfect Continuous tense is used for actions that were ongoing before another action in the past.

Hinglish Explanation

पास्ट परफेक्ट कंटीन्यूउस उन कामों के लिए use होता है जो past में किसी दूसरे काम से पहले चल रहे थे।

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + had + been + Verb+ing
Negative: Subject + had + not + been + Verb+ing
Interrogative: Had + Subject + been + Verb+ing?

Helping Verbs

had been

Signal Words

for, since, how long, before, when

Examples

  • I had been studying for two hours before dinner.
  • She had been working there since 2018.
  • They had been playing cricket for hours.
  • We had been waiting for you since morning.
  • He had been reading that book for weeks.
  • It had been raining since yesterday.
  • You had been sleeping for eight hours.
  • The children had been watching TV all day.

Common Mistakes

Wrong: I had been knowing him for years.

Right: I had known him for years.

Why: Stative verbs (know, like, love) don't use continuous forms.

Simple Future

Used for decisions and predictions about the future

Future Continuous

Used for actions in progress at a specific future time

Future Perfect

Used for actions that will be completed before a future time

Future Perfect Continuous

Used for ongoing actions before a future time

Definition

Simple Future tense is used for decisions made at the moment of speaking and predictions about the future.

Hinglish Explanation

सिंपल फ्यूचर उन कामों के लिए use होता है जो भविष्य में होंगे या अभी decide किए गए हों।

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + will + Base Verb
Negative: Subject + will + not + Base Verb
Interrogative: Will + Subject + Base Verb?

Helping Verbs

will, shall (shall is less common now)

Signal Words

tomorrow, next week, soon, in 2025, later, tonight

Examples

  • I will call you tomorrow.
  • She will study medicine.
  • They will arrive next week.
  • We will help you with your project.
  • He will buy a new car.
  • It will rain tonight.
  • You will love this movie.
  • The meeting will start at 3 PM.

Common Mistakes

Wrong: I will going to school tomorrow.

Right: I will go to school tomorrow.

Why: After 'will', always use base form of verb.

Definition

Future Continuous tense is used for actions that will be in progress at a specific time in the future.

Hinglish Explanation

फ्यूचर कंटीन्यूउस उन कामों के लिए use होता है जो भविष्य में किसी खास समय चल रहे होंगे।

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + will + be + Verb+ing
Negative: Subject + will + not + be + Verb+ing
Interrogative: Will + Subject + be + Verb+ing?

Helping Verbs

will be

Signal Words

at this time tomorrow, at 8 PM tonight, when you arrive

Examples

  • I will be studying at 8 PM tonight.
  • She will be sleeping when you arrive.
  • They will be working tomorrow morning.
  • We will be traveling this time next week.
  • He will be waiting for you at the station.
  • It will be raining heavily.
  • You will be enjoying your vacation.
  • The children will be playing in the park.

Common Mistakes

Wrong: I will be go to school at 8 AM.

Right: I will be going to school at 8 AM.

Why: After 'will be', use verb+ing form.

Definition

Future Perfect tense is used for actions that will be completed before a specific time in the future.

Hinglish Explanation

फ्यूचर परफेक्ट उन कामों के लिए use होता है जो भविष्य में किसी समय से पहले पूरे हो जाएंगे।

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + will + have + Past Participle
Negative: Subject + will + not + have + Past Participle
Interrogative: Will + Subject + have + Past Participle?

Helping Verbs

will have

Signal Words

by, by the time, before, until, when

Examples

  • I will have finished my homework by 8 PM.
  • She will have completed her degree by 2025.
  • They will have arrived before dinner.
  • We will have saved enough money by December.
  • He will have read the book by tomorrow.
  • By next year, I will have learned English.
  • She will have cooked dinner by the time you arrive.
  • The train will have left by 6 AM.

Common Mistakes

Wrong: I will have went to school by 9 AM.

Right: I will have gone to school by 9 AM.

Why: After 'will have', use past participle (gone, not went).

Definition

Future Perfect Continuous tense is used for actions that will be ongoing up to a specific time in the future.

Hinglish Explanation

फ्यूचर परफेक्ट कंटीन्यूउस उन कामों के लिए use होता है जो भविष्य में किसी समय तक चल रहे होंगे।

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + will + have + been + Verb+ing
Negative: Subject + will + not + have + been + Verb+ing
Interrogative: Will + Subject + have + been + Verb+ing?

Helping Verbs

will have been

Signal Words

for, by, by the time, when

Examples

  • I will have been working here for five years by 2025.
  • She will have been studying for three hours by 8 PM.
  • They will have been living in Mumbai for a decade.
  • We will have been waiting for two hours by then.
  • He will have been teaching for 20 years.
  • It will have been raining for hours by the time you arrive.
  • You will have been learning English for six months.
  • The children will have been playing all day.

Common Mistakes

Wrong: I will have been work here for five years.

Right: I will have been working here for five years.

Why: After 'will have been', use verb+ing form.
