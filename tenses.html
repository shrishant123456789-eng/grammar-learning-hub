Present Simple Tense

1️⃣ Definition

We use Present Simple for things that happen every day or always. Example: “I eat bread every morning.”

2️⃣ Hinglish Explanation

Jab bhi koi kaam jo roz, hamesha, ya aksar hota hai—jaise “School 7 baje khulta hai” ya “Main roz brush karta hoon”—to Present Simple bolte hain. Is tense mein time fix hota hai: daily, usually, always, never, every week, etc.

3️⃣ Structure / Formula

AffirmativeSubject + Verb-1 (s/es with he/she/it)
NegativeSubject + do/does + not + Verb-1
InterrogativeDo/Does + Subject + Verb-1 ?

4️⃣ Helping Verbs

Do/Does. ‘Does’ for he/she/it; ‘Do’ for I/you/we/they.

5️⃣ Signal Words

every day, always, usually, often, sometimes, never, on Mondays

6️⃣ Examples

  • She drinks milk daily.
  • They play football on Fridays.
  • The sun rises in the east.
  • He does not like cold coffee.
  • Do you read newspapers?
  • Water boils at 100 °C.
  • My father drives carefully.
  • Birds fly high.
  • We visit grandma every Sunday.
  • It rarely rains here.

7️⃣ Common Mistakes

Wrong: She play tennis.
Right: She plays tennis. (Add ‘s’)

Present Continuous Tense

1️⃣ Definition

We use Present Continuous for things happening right now. Example: “I am eating lunch.”

2️⃣ Hinglish Explanation

Abhi, isi waqt, jo kaam chal raha hai—jaise “Main abhi khana kha raha hoon”—Present Continuous bolte hain. ‘Now, at the moment, currently’ jaise words iske signal hain.

3️⃣ Structure / Formula

AffirmativeSubject + am/is/are + Verb-ing
NegativeSubject + am/is/are + not + Verb-ing
InterrogativeAm/Is/Are + Subject + Verb-ing ?

4️⃣ Helping Verbs

am/is/are. ‘am’ with I; ‘is’ with he/she/it; ‘are’ with we/you/they.

5️⃣ Signal Words

now, right now, at the moment, currently, Look!, Listen!

6️⃣ Examples

  • She is reading a novel.
  • They are waiting for the bus.
  • I am typing an email.
  • He is not watching TV.
  • Are you sleeping?
  • The baby is crying loudly.
  • We are learning tenses.
  • It is raining outside.
  • The students are singing.
  • Look! The dog is running.

7️⃣ Common Mistakes

Wrong: I working now.
Right: I am working now. (Helping verb needed)

Present Perfect Tense

1️⃣ Definition

We use Present Perfect for finished actions that are important now. Example: “I have eaten.”

2️⃣ Hinglish Explanation

Jo kaam abhi khatam hua ho aur uska result abhi zaroori ho—jaise “Maine kaam khatam kar liya hai” (abhi free hoon)—Present Perfect use karte hain. ‘Just, already, yet, ever, never’ jaise words iske signals hain.

3️⃣ Structure / Formula

AffirmativeSubject + have/has + Verb-3
NegativeSubject + have/has + not + Verb-3
InterrogativeHave/Has + Subject + Verb-3 ?

4️⃣ Helping Verbs

have/has. ‘has’ with he/she/it; ‘have’ with I/you/we/they.

5️⃣ Signal Words

just, already, yet, ever, never, so far, recently

6️⃣ Examples

  • She has finished her homework.
  • I have visited Delhi twice.
  • We have seen that movie.
  • He has not replied yet.
  • Have you eaten lunch?
  • They have bought a new car.
  • The train has left.
  • It has just started to rain.
  • My parents have arrived safely.
  • I have never flown in a helicopter.

7️⃣ Common Mistakes

Wrong: I have saw him.
Right: I have seen him. (Use Verb-3)

Present Perfect Continuous Tense

1️⃣ Definition

We use Present Perfect Continuous for actions that started in the past and are still continuing. Example: “I have been studying for two hours.”

2️⃣ Hinglish Explanation

Jo kaam guzishta time se chala aa raha ho aur abhi bhi chal raha ho—jaise “Do ghante se padh raha hoon” (abhi bhi padh raha hoon)—Present Perfect Continuous bolte hain. ‘For + duration, since + point of time’ iske pehchan hain.

3️⃣ Structure / Formula

AffirmativeSubject + have/has + been + Verb-ing
NegativeSubject + have/has + not + been + Verb-ing
InterrogativeHave/Has + Subject + been + Verb-ing ?

4️⃣ Helping Verbs

have/has + been

5️⃣ Signal Words

for two hours, since morning, how long, all day, lately

6️⃣ Examples

  • She has been cooking since 7 am.
  • I have been waiting for the bus for twenty minutes.
  • We have been living here for five years.
  • He has not been sleeping well lately.
  • Have you been studying all night?
  • They have been playing football since school time.
  • It has been raining for hours.
  • The baby has been crying non-stop.
  • I have been working on this project for a week.
  • How long have you been learning English?

7️⃣ Common Mistakes

Right: I have been working here since 2018.

Past Simple Tense

1️⃣ Definition

We use Past Simple for actions that finished in the past. Example: “I watched a movie yesterday.”

2️⃣ Hinglish Explanation

Jo kaam kal, pichle hafte, ya kisi fix past time pe hua—jaise “Kal maine movie dekhi”—Past Simple bolte hain. ‘Yesterday, last night, in 2010’ jaise words iske signals hain.

3️⃣ Structure / Formula

AffirmativeSubject + Verb-2
NegativeSubject + did + not + Verb-1
InterrogativeDid + Subject + Verb-1 ?

4️⃣ Helping Verbs

did (for negative & questions)

5️⃣ Signal Words

yesterday, last week, two days ago, in 1999, then

6️⃣ Examples

  • She visited Delhi last month.
  • We finished the project on time.
  • I did not eat pizza yesterday.

  • They travelled by train.
  • He studied hard for the exam.
  • The movie started at 8 pm.
  • I lost my keys in the market.
  • She bought a new dress.

7️⃣ Common Mistakes

Past Perfect Tense

1️⃣ Definition

We use Past Perfect for the earlier of two past actions. Example: “I had left before he arrived.”

2️⃣ Hinglish Explanation

Jab do past actions ho aur pata karna ho kaunsi pehle hui—pehle wali Past Perfect, baad wali Past Simple. “Train ja chuki thi jab main station pahuncha.”

3️⃣ Structure / Formula

AffirmativeSubject + had + Verb-3
NegativeSubject + had + not + Verb-3
InterrogativeHad + Subject + Verb-3 ?

4️⃣ Helping Verbs

had

5️⃣ Signal Words

already, just, never, before, by the time, after

6️⃣ Examples

  • She had finished lunch before 2 pm.
  • We had seen the movie earlier.
  • I had not met him before the party.
  • Had you booked the tickets?
  • They had left when we arrived.
  • He had already eaten, so he was not hungry.
  • The guests had gone before sunset.
  • I had lost my keys, so I couldn’t open the door.
  • She had written three letters.
  • We had never visited Jaipur before.

7️⃣ Common Mistakes

Wrong: I had ate.
Right: I had eaten. (Use Verb-3)

Future Simple Tense

1️⃣ Definition

We use Future Simple for decisions or predictions about the future. Example: “I will help you tomorrow.”

2️⃣ Hinglish Explanation

Kal, parsaon, ya kisi bhi future time pe jo kaam karne wale hain—jaise “Kal main tumhari madad karunga”—Future Simple bolte hain. ‘Will’ ka use karte hain.

3️⃣ Structure / Formula

AffirmativeSubject + will + Verb-1
NegativeSubject + will + not + Verb-1
InterrogativeWill + Subject + Verb-1 ?

4️⃣ Helping Verbs

will/shall (shall with I/we in formal English)

5️⃣ Signal Words

tomorrow, next week, soon, later, in two days

6️⃣ Examples

  • She will travel to Chennai tomorrow.
  • We will start the meeting soon.
  • I will not forget your help.
  • Will you join us for lunch?
  • They will build a new hospital.
  • He will call you later.
  • It will probably rain tonight.
  • We shall overcome the problem.
  • I will finish the report by evening.
  • Who will drive the car?

7️⃣ Common Mistakes

Wrong: I will going.
Right: I will go. (Verb-1 after will)
