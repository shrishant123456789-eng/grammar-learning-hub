Welcome to Speaking Vocabulary


🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

"Vocabulary ka matlab sirf dictionary padhna nahi hota. Problem ye hai ki humein words ka meaning pata hota hai, par jab bolne ki baari aati hai toh words yaad nahi aate. Is module ka goal hai—words ko sentences ke saath seekhna taaki wo muh se naturally nikle."

Simple Logic: Agar aapke paas 'Building Blocks' (Words) nahi hain, toh 'Design' (Grammar) kitna bhi accha ho, ghar (Speaking) nahi banega.

🇬🇧 English Explanation

Speaking vocabulary is different from reading vocabulary. Native speakers reuse simple words like "get," "take," "make," and "have" in hundreds of ways. You don't need difficult words to sound fluent; you need to know how to use simple words effectively.

⚠️ Important Rules for This Module:

  • Don't memorize meanings alone. Memorize the **whole sentence**.
  • Focus on Verbs (Action) and Adjectives (Quality) first.
  • Stop translating word-for-word from Hindi. Think of the **situation**.
