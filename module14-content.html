What is Real Conversation?

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

Hello Friends! Conversation ka matlab sirf sawal ka jawab dena nahi hota.
Agar koi pooche "How are you?" aur aap bas bolo "Fine", toh conversation wahi khatam ho jati hai.

Real Conversation ka matlab hai: Apna opinion dena, saamne wale ki baat par react karna, aur nayi details add karke baat ko aage badhana.

🇬🇧 English Explanation

A conversation is a two-way street. It involves Listening + Thinking + Responding + Adding Value.

  • Don't just answer: Add a "Because" (Reason).
  • Don't be a robot: Show emotion (Shock, Happiness, Doubt).
  • Don't stop: Ask a follow-up question to the other person.

💡 Key Rules for this Module:

  • No One-Word Answers: Never say just "Yes" or "No".
  • Use Fillers: It's okay to say "Well...", "Actually...", "Let me think..."
  • Click the Speaker Button: Listen to the tone, not just the words.

⚠️ Common Mistakes by Indian Learners

Mistake 1: The "Interview" Mode

Wrong: "My name is Raj. I am from Delhi. I like cricket." (Too robotic)

Correct: "Hi, I'm Raj! I've been living in Delhi for 5 years now, and honestly, I'm a huge cricket fan."

Mistake 2: Fear of Silence

Problem: You panic when you don't know the word.

Solution: Use 'Thinking phrases'.

Example: "That’s a good question... let me think about it for a second."

Mistake 3: Disagreeing Rudely

Wrong: "No, you are wrong."

Correct: "I see your point, but I have a slightly different opinion."
