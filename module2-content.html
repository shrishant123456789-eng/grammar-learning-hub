📖 What is 'Parts of Speech'?

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation:

“Jab hum koi tasty dish banate hain, toh humein ingredients chahiye hote hain (jaise namak, paani, sabzi). Waise hi, English sentence banane ke liye humein 'Parts of Speech' chahiye. Is module me hum seekhenge ki sentence ke andar word ka role kya hota hai – kaun kaam kar raha hai (Noun), kya kaam ho raha hai (Verb), aur cheezein kahan rakhi hain (Preposition).”

🇬🇧 English Explanation:

Parts of Speech are the building blocks of a sentence. Just like a house needs bricks, cement, and wood, a sentence needs Nouns, Verbs, and Adjectives. In this module, you will learn how to put these blocks together to speak simple, correct sentences.

⚡ Key Takeaways:
  • Noun: The name of a person, place, or thing (The "Hero" of the sentence).
  • Pronoun: Used in place of a Noun (I, You, He, She) to avoid repetition.
  • Verb: The action word (Run, Eat) or state (Is, Am, Are).
  • Adjective: Adds detail or description (Good, Bad, Red, Happy).
  • Preposition: Shows location or time (In, On, At).

▶ Topic 1: Introduction to Parts of Speech

Think of a sentence like a cricket team. Every player has a role. Some bat, some bowl.

Sentence me har word ka ek kaam hota hai. Koi 'Naam' batata hai, koi 'Kaam' batata hai, aur koi 'Taareef' karta hai. In roles ko hi Parts of Speech kehte hain.

Why is this important? If you put the bowler in the wrong place, you lose. If you put words in the wrong place, the meaning changes!

▶ Topic 2: Noun (Spoken Use)

Definition: Naming words. Names of people, places, things, or ideas.

Aan-baan aur shaan se yaad rakho: Duniya me jis bhi cheez ka naam hai, wo Noun hai. Example: Rahul, Delhi, Mobile, Love.

  • Person: Rahul, Doctor, Mom.
  • Place: India, Kitchen, Office.
  • Thing: Phone, Water, Car.
▶ Topic 3: Pronoun (Spoken Use)

Definition: Words used instead of a Noun.

Baar-baar naam lena ajeeb lagta hai. "Rahul is good. Rahul is happy." No! Hum bolte hain: "Rahul is good. He is happy."

▶ Topic 4: Verb (Action Words)

Definition: The engine of the sentence. It shows Action or State.

1. Action Verbs: Run, Eat, Sleep, Talk.
2. Helping Verbs (State): Is, Am, Are, Was, Were.

Bina Verb ke sentence nahi banta.
Wrong: "I happy." (Isme action/state missing hai).
Correct: "I am happy." (Am = Verb).

▶ Topic 5: Adjective (Describing Words)

Definition: Words that describe a Noun.

Ye noun ki 'visheshata' (quality) batate hain. Kaisa hai? Achha, bura, lamba, chhota.

Examples:

  • She is a smart girl.
  • The tea is hot.
  • It is a big house.
▶ Topic 6: Prepositions (in / on / at)

These are very confusing for beginners. Let's simplify.

IN (Mein/Andar): Jab koi cheez kisi boundary ke andar ho. (In the room, In India).

ON (Par/Upar): Jab cheez surface touch kar rahi ho. (On the table, On the wall).

AT (Specific Point): Kisi specific location ya time par. (At 5 PM, At the bus stop).

▶ Topic 7: Spoken Sentence Building

Combine everything now!

[Pronoun] + [Verb] + [Preposition] + [Noun]

Examples:

  • He (Pro) + is sitting (Verb) + on (Prep) + the chair (Noun).
  • I (Pro) + live (Verb) + in (Prep) + Delhi (Noun).
▶ Topic 8: Common Indian Mistakes
❌ Wrong ✅ Correct
He is in home. He is at home.
I am agree. I agree.
She is more tall. She is taller.
Marriage anniversary. Wedding anniversary.
▶ Topic 9: Confidence Tips

🗣️ Speak Yourself - Daily Practice

Stand in front of a mirror and say these 5 sentences loudly:

  1. I am a learner.
  2. My English is improving.
  3. I am sitting on a chair.
  4. The fan is above me.
  5. I am confident.

Tip: Don't fear grammar. Even if you say "I go market" instead of "I am going to market", people will understand. Start speaking!
