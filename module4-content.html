1️⃣ Self-Introduction (Khud ka Parichay)

"Jab aap kisi se pehli baar milte hain, toh impression important hota hai. Sirf naam batana kaafi nahi hai. Hum seekhenge ki professional aur friendly tareeke se intro kaise de."

📌 Standard Introduction Structure

  • Greeting: Hello / Good Morning.
  • Name: I am [Name] / My name is [Name].
  • Place: I am from [City] / I live in [City].
  • Job/Study: I work as a [Job] / I am a student.
  • Hobby: I like [Hobby].

🗣️ Daily Spoken Sentences

SituationEnglish Sentence
Informal greetingHi, I'm Rahul. Nice to meet you.
Formal greetingGood morning, my name is Priya Sharma.
Asking identityMay I know your name, please?
Telling professionI work at an IT company in Pune.
Telling originBasically, I am from Delhi, but I live in Mumbai.

4️⃣ Asking Questions Politely

Golden Rule: Avoid direct orders like "Give me pen." Use "Could you..." or "May I..."

📌 Magic Words for Questions

  • Could you please...? (Requesting help)
  • Would you like...? (Offering something)
  • May I...? (Asking permission)
  • Do you mind...? (Checking comfort)

🗣️ Polite vs Rude

❌ Rude / Direct✅ Polite / Natural
Sit down.Please have a seat.
Give me water.Could I have a glass of water, please?
What is your name?May I know your name?
Wait here.Could you please wait for a moment?
I want tea.I would like to have some tea.

5️⃣ Giving Short Answers

"Sirf 'Yes' ya 'No' bolna kabhi-kabhi rude lagta hai. English me hum auxiliary verb add karte hain."

📌 The Pattern

Question: Do you like cricket?
Answer: Yes, I do. (Not just 'Yes')

Question: Are you coming?
Answer: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

🗣️ Conversation Examples

QuestionShort Answer
Did you call him?Yes, I did.
Will you help me?Yes, I will.
Is he your brother?No, he isn't.
Have you eaten?No, I haven't.
Can you drive?Yes, I can.

6️⃣ Spoken Sentence Patterns

Memorize these chunks to speak faster without thinking about grammar.

  • I am fond of... (Mujhe shauk hai...)
    Example: I am fond of cooking.
  • I am planning to... (Soch raha hoon...)
    Example: I am planning to buy a car.
  • Let me... (Mujhe karne do...)
    Example: Let me check my schedule.
  • How about... (Kaisa rahega?)
    Example: How about a coffee?
  • It seems that... (Aisa lagta hai ki...)
    Example: It seems that it will rain.

8️⃣ Confidence & Body Language

"Communication is 7% words, 38% tone, and 55% body language."

🚀 Confidence Tips

  • Eye Contact: Look at the person, not the floor.
  • Smile: It makes you sound friendly and confident.
  • Don't Rush: Speaking slowly shows you are in control.
  • Hands: Use open hand gestures. Don't cross arms.
  • Filler Words: Avoid excessive "uhhh", "ummm". Take a pause instead.
