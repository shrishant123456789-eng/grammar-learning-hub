Parts of Speech – Overview

Basic to Advanced Grammar Notes with Practice

📘 What is Parts of Speech?

Parts of Speech are categories of words based on their function in a sentence.

English has 8 main Parts of Speech.

📗 The 8 Parts of Speech

  1. Noun – Name of a person, place, thing
  2. Pronoun – Used instead of noun
  3. Verb – Shows action or state
  4. Adjective – Describes a noun
  5. Adverb – Describes verb/adjective
  6. Preposition – Shows relation
  7. Conjunction – Joins words/sentences
  8. Interjection – Expresses emotion

📙 Example Sentence

Ram (Noun) quickly (Adverb) ate (Verb) a sweet (Adjective) mango.
