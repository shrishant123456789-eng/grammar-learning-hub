The Secret of Spoken Grammar

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

Problem: Aapko saare rules pata hain (Noun, Pronoun, Verb), phir bhi bolte waqt sentence nahi banta. Kyun?

Reason: Kyunki aap grammar ko "Exam Subject" ki tarah treat karte ho. Aap rule yaad karte ho, phir translate karte ho.

Solution: Spoken English mein grammar ka matlab hai Patterns. Jaise gaadi chalate waqt hum engine ke baare mein nahi sochte, waise hi bolte waqt rule ke baare mein mat socho. Bas pattern follow karo.

🇬🇧 Spoken Grammar Principles

  • Function over Form: It matters more why you are saying it, not just strict rules.
  • Short & Clear: A short, grammatically correct sentence is better than a long, confusing one.
  • Flexible: In spoken English, "Long time no see" is correct, even if textbooks say otherwise.
