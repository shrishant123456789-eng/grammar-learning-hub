The "Robot vs. Human" Theory

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

Bahut saare students ki grammar perfect hoti hai, lekin jab wo bolte hain toh Computer ya News Reader jaise lagte hain.

Natural English ka matlab hai: Perfect grammar nahi, balki Flow. Native speakers har word ko alag-alag nahi bolte, wo words ko jodte hain (link), short forms use karte hain, aur beech mein 'Fillers' use karte hain jab wo soch rahe hote hain.

🇬🇧 What is Natural English?

  • Correct English: "I am going to want to eat a pizza." (Too stiff)
  • Natural English: "I'm actually gonna grab a pizza." (Human)

In this module, we will break your "Robotic Habits" and install "Human Software".
