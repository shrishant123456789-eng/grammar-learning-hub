Subject and Predicate

Complete Notes + Practice (Basic to Advanced)

📘 What is a Sentence?

A sentence is a group of words that makes complete sense.

📗 Subject

The Subject is the part of the sentence about which something is said. It usually tells who or what the sentence is about.

Example:

Ram is playing.

📕 Predicate

The Predicate is the part of the sentence that tells something about the subject. It contains the verb and the remaining part of the sentence.

Example:

Ram is playing.

📌 Rule

  • Every sentence has a subject and a predicate.
  • The subject comes first in most sentences.
  • The predicate always contains a verb.
