🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation (Confidence Booster)

Fluency ka matlab tez bolna nahi hota. Bahut se learners sochte hain ki agar wo machine gun ki tarah English bolenge toh wo fluent lagenge. Yeh galat hai.

Fluency ka asli matlab hai: Smoothness.

Jab aapke thoughts bina rukawat ke flow karte hain, jab aapko Hindi se translate nahi karna padta, aur jab aap galti hone ke dar se rukte nahi hain — use kehte hain Fluency.

🇬🇧 English Definition

Fluency is the ability to express thoughts easily and articulately. It's about connecting ideas, not just words.

  • Don't focus on Speed: Focus on clarity and pauses.
  • Don't translate: Think directly in the target language.
  • Accept Mistakes: Even native speakers make grammar mistakes. They just don't stop talking.
