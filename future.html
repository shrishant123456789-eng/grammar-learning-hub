Definition

We use the Simple Future Tense to talk about actions that will happen at a later time. It is used for predictions, promises, or instant decisions.

Hinglish Explanation:
Hum Simple Future ka use tab karte hain jab hum future me hone wale kisi kaam ke baare me baat karte hain. Jaise: "Main kal jaunga" ya "Woh aayega". Isme hum aksar "ga, ge, gi" wale sentences sochte hain.

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + will + V1 (Base Form) + Object
Negative: Subject + will not (won't) + V1 + Object
Interrogative: Will + Subject + V1 + Object?

Helping Verbs

Will: Used with all subjects (I, You, He, She, It, We, They).
Note: 'Shall' is sometimes used with I/We in very formal English, but 'Will' is standard for everything now.

Signal Words

Tomorrow, next week, soon, later, in the future, tonight, in 2030.

Examples

  • I will call you tomorrow. (Promise)
  • She will help us with the project. (Prediction/Fact)
  • They will not come to the party. (Negative)
  • Will you marry me? (Question)
  • Wait, I will open the door. (Instant decision)

Common Mistakes

❌ I will going to the market.
✔ I will go to the market.
👉 After 'will', always use V1 (Base Form). Do not use 'ing' without 'be'.
❌ She will goes to school.
✔ She will go to school.
👉 Never add 's' or 'es' with the verb after 'will'.
❌ I will to play cricket.
✔ I will play cricket.
👉 Do not use 'to' after 'will'.

Mini Practice (Simple Future)

Definition

Used to describe an action that will be in progress (happening) at a specific time in the future.

Hinglish Explanation:
Iska use tab karte hain jab future me kisi khaas samay par koi kaam chal raha hoga. Jaise: "Kal is waqt main padh raha hounga" (I will be studying).

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + will be + V1 + ing + Object
Negative: Subject + will not be + V1 + ing...
Interrogative: Will + Subject + be + V1 + ing...?

Examples

  • At 10 AM tomorrow, I will be sleeping.
  • They will be playing football on Sunday evening.
  • She will not be working next week.
  • Will you be coming with us?
  • Don't call him now, he will be driving.

Common Mistakes

❌ I will be sleep.
✔ I will be sleeping.
👉 Continuous tense always needs 'ing'.
❌ She will sleeping.
✔ She will be sleeping.
👉 Don't forget 'be' after 'will' in continuous tense.

Mini Practice (Future Continuous)

Definition

Used for actions that will be completed (finished) by a certain time in the future.

Hinglish Explanation:
Jab humein batana ho ki future me kisi time tak koi kaam khatam ho chuka hoga. Isme aksar "by" (tak) ka use hota hai. Jaise: "Kal tak main apna kaam khatam kar chuka hounga."

Structure / Formula

Formula: Subject + will have + V3 (Past Participle) + Object
V3 Example: Go → Gone, Finish → Finished.

Signal Words

By tomorrow, by next week, by the time, by 2025.

Examples

  • I will have finished this book by tomorrow.
  • She will have left before you arrive.
  • They will have graduated by next year.
  • We will have eaten dinner by 8 PM.

Common Mistakes

❌ He will has finished.
✔ He will have finished.
👉 With 'will', we always use 'have', never 'has' (even for He/She).
❌ I will have finish.
✔ I will have finished.
👉 Always use V3 (3rd form) in Perfect Tenses.

Mini Practice (Future Perfect)

Definition

Used to describe an action that will continue up until a point in the future. It emphasizes duration.

Hinglish Explanation:
Jab koi kaam pehle se chal raha ho aur future me kisi point tak chalta rahega. Isme hum duration (samay seema) par zor dete hain. Jaise: "Agley saal mujhe yahan kaam karte hue 10 saal ho jayenge."

Structure / Formula

Formula: Subject + will have been + V1 + ing + (Since/For + Time)

Examples

  • By 5 PM, I will have been studying for 4 hours.
  • Next year, they will have been living here for a decade.
  • She will have been working since morning when you reach.

Common Mistakes

❌ I will have be studying.
✔ I will have been studying.
👉 The correct helper is 'will have been'.
