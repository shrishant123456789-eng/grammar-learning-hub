ARTICLES (A, AN, THE)

Complete Grammar Chapter with Practice & Tests

① WHAT ARE ARTICLES?

📚 Definition

Articles are words that define a noun as specific or unspecific. They come before nouns to show whether we are referring to something specific or general.

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

Articles wo shabd hote hain jo noun ke pehle aate hain aur batate hain ki wo noun general hai ya specific. Hindi mein iska koi direct equivalent nahin hai, lekin ye "ek" ya "wo" ke jaise kaam karte hain.

✅ Correct Examples

A boy is playing. (General - koi bhi ladka)

An apple is red. (General - koi bhi apple)

The sun is hot. (Specific - hum sab jaante hain kaun sa sun)

I saw a dog. (General - koi bhi dog)

The dog was black. (Specific - jo dog maine dekha)

❌ Wrong Examples

✗ I want a water. (Wrong because water uncountable hai)

✗ She is a honest girl. (Wrong because 'honest' vowel sound se shuru hota hai)

✗ I went to the Delhi. (Wrong because city names ke saath 'the' nahin lagta)

② TYPES OF ARTICLES

🔍 Indefinite Articles

A / An

Use: Jab hum kisi general cheez ki baat karte hain

Example: A boy, An apple

📍 Definite Article

The

Use: Jab hum kisi specific cheez ki baat karte hain

Example: The sun, The book I read

❌ Zero Article

No article

Use: Kuch specific cases mein article lagana wrong hota hai

Example: I love music (not 'the music')

Type When to Use Examples Hindi Meaning
A Consonant sound ke pehle a boy, a cat, a university Ek (general)
An Vowel sound ke pehle an apple, an hour, an honest man Ek (general)
The Specific things ke liye the sun, the book, the Ganga Wo specific
Zero Proper nouns, abstract nouns Delhi, love, music Bina article

③ A – DETAILED RULES

Rule 1: Before Consonant Sound

✔ Correct: A boy, A cat, A dog, A pen

❌ Common Mistake: "A apple" (wrong because apple starts with vowel sound)

✔ Correct: "An apple"

Why wrong: Hum alphabet dekh kar decide nahin karte, sound sun kar karte hain!

Rule 2: Before Words Like University, European, One

✔ Correct: A university, A European, A one-way street

❌ Common Mistake: "An university" (kyunki university 'u' se shuru hota hai)

✔ Correct: "A university"

Why: University ka pronunciation "Yuniversity" hota hai - 'Y' consonant sound hai!

Rule 3: Before Singular Countable Nouns

✔ Correct: I need a pen. She bought a dress.

❌ Common Mistake: "I need pen" (article missing)

✔ Correct: "I need a pen"

Why: Singular countable noun ke sahe hamesha article lagta hai!

Rule 4: With Professions

✔ Correct: He is a doctor. She wants to be a teacher.

❌ Common Mistake: "He is doctor"

✔ Correct: "He is a doctor"

Why: Profession batate waqt hamesha 'a' lagate hain!

SSC TIP: "A" ka matlab hai "ek" - general sense mein. Jab aap kisi specific cheez ki baat nahin kar rahe, use "a"!

④ AN – DETAILED RULES

Rule 1: Before Vowel Sound (A, E, I, O, U)

✔ Correct: An apple, An elephant, An ice-cream, An orange, An umbrella

❌ Common Mistake: "A apple"

✔ Correct: "An apple"

Why wrong: Apple vowel sound se shuru hota hai - 'an' lagna chahiye!

Rule 2: Before Words Like Honest, Hour, Heir

✔ Correct: An honest man, An hour, An heir

❌ Common Mistake: "A honest man" (kyunki 'h' consonant hai)

✔ Correct: "An honest man"

Why: In words mein 'h' silent hota hai - sound 'o' se shuru hota hai!

Rule 3: Before Abbreviations (MBA, FIR, MLA)

✔ Correct: An MBA degree, An FIR, An MLA

❌ Common Mistake: "A MBA degree"

✔ Correct: "An MBA degree"

Why: MBA ka pronunciation "Em-Bee-A" hota hai - 'Em' vowel sound hai!

Rule 4: With 'H' Words Where H is Silent

✔ Correct: An hour, An honest boy, An heir, An honor

❌ Common Mistake: "A hour"

✔ Correct: "An hour"

Why: Hour ka pronunciation "our" hota hai - 'o' vowel sound hai!

SSC TIP: Sound pe dhyaan do, letter pe nahin! "An honest" lekin "A house" (kyunki house mein 'h' bolte hain)!

⑤ THE – DETAILED RULES

Rule 1: Unique Things in the World

✔ Correct: The sun, The moon, The earth, The sky, The world

❌ Common Mistake: "Sun is hot"

✔ Correct: "The sun is hot"

Why: Sun ek hi hai duniya mein - unique hai!

Rule 2: Rivers, Seas, Oceans

✔ Correct: The Ganga, The Yamuna, The Pacific Ocean, The Red Sea

❌ Common Mistake: "I swam in Ganga"

✔ Correct: "I swam in the Ganga"

Why: Rivers ke saath hamesha 'the' lagta hai!

Rule 3: Mountain Ranges

✔ Correct: The Himalayas, The Alps, The Andes

❌ Common Mistake: "I visited Himalayas"

✔ Correct: "I visited the Himalayas"

Why: Mountain ranges (plural) ke saath 'the' lagta hai!

Rule 4: Superlatives

✔ Correct: The best, The tallest, The most beautiful

❌ Common Mistake: "She is best student"

✔ Correct: "She is the best student"

Why: Superlative degree ke saath hamesha 'the' lagta hai!

Rule 5: Musical Instruments

✔ Correct: I play the guitar, She learns the piano

❌ Common Mistake: "I play guitar"

✔ Correct: "I play the guitar"

Why: Musical instruments ke saath 'the' lagta hai!

Rule 6: Newspapers

✔ Correct: The Times of India, The Hindu, The Indian Express

❌ Common Mistake: "I read Times of India"

✔ Correct: "I read the Times of India"

Why: Newspapers ke names ke saath 'the' lagta hai!

Rule 7: Historical Buildings

✔ Correct: The Taj Mahal, The Red Fort, The Qutub Minar

❌ Common Mistake: "I visited Taj Mahal"

✔ Correct: "I visited the Taj Mahal"

Why: Famous buildings ke saath 'the' lagta hai!

Rule 8: Before Adjective (the poor, the rich)

✔ Correct: The poor, The rich, The unemployed, The disabled

❌ Common Mistake: "Poor need help"

✔ Correct: "The poor need help"

Why: Adjective ke saath 'the' lagane se group of people indicate hota hai!

SSC TIP: "The" ka matlab hai "wo specific" - jo hum dono jaante hain! Jab uniqueness ho, use "the"!

⑥ ZERO ARTICLE

Rule 1: Before Plural Nouns (General Sense)

✔ Correct: Dogs are loyal. Cats are cute. Birds fly.

❌ Common Mistake: "The dogs are loyal" (jab general baat kar rahe ho)

✔ Correct: "Dogs are loyal" (general statement)

Why: General statements mein plural nouns ke saath koi article nahin lagta!

Rule 2: Before Abstract Nouns

✔ Correct: Love is blind. Honesty is best. Life is beautiful.

❌ Common Mistake: "The love is blind"

✔ Correct: "Love is blind"

Why: Abstract nouns ke saath generally koi article nahin lagta!

Rule 3: Before Languages

✔ Correct: I speak Hindi. She learns English. They know French.

❌ Common Mistake: "I speak the Hindi"

✔ Correct: "I speak Hindi"

Why: Languages ke saath koi article nahin lagta!

Rule 4: Before Meals

✔ Correct: I eat breakfast. Let's have lunch. Dinner is ready.

❌ Common Mistake: "I eat the breakfast"

✔ Correct: "I eat breakfast"

Why: Meals ke saath generally koi article nahin lagta!

Rule 5: Before Proper Nouns

✔ Correct: I live in Delhi. She goes to Mumbai. Rahul is my friend.

❌ Common Mistake: "I live in the Delhi"

✔ Correct: "I live in Delhi"

Why: City names, person names ke saath koi article nahin lagta!

SSC TIP: Zero article ka matlab hai "kuch bhi nahin"! Jab general baat ho, abstract cheez ho, ya proper noun ho - kuch nahin lagana!
