Articles – A, An, The

Basic to Advanced | Clear Rules | Practice with Explanation

📘 What is an Article?

An article is a word used before a noun to show whether the noun is general or specific.

📗 Types of Articles

1. A
Used before a word beginning with a consonant sound.
a boy, a pen, a university
2. An
Used before a word beginning with a vowel sound.
an apple, an hour, an honest man
3. The
Used to refer to a particular or specific person or thing.
the sun, the Taj Mahal, the boy you met

📙 Important Rules (Exam Focus)

• Use a / an for first mention, the for second mention.
• Use an with vowel sound, not vowel letter.
• Use the with unique things, rivers, seas, mountains.
• No article before plural or abstract nouns when used generally.
