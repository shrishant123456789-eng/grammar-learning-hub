Articles – A, An, The

Complete Notes + Practice (Basic to Advanced)

📘 What is an Article?

Article is a word used before a noun to limit or define its meaning.

There are three Articles in English: A, An, The

📗 Types of Articles

  • Indefinite Articles: A, An
  • Definite Article: The

📕 Rules of A

A is used before words starting with a consonant sound.
A boy, A cat, A university (yu sound)

📕 Rules of An

An is used before words starting with a vowel sound.
An apple, An honest man, An hour

📕 Rules of The

The is used for specific person/place/thing or something already known.
The sun, The Ganga, The book you gave me

⚠ Important Points

  • Article depends on sound, not spelling
  • No article used with plural/general nouns
  • No article before names of persons
