Sentence

Created by Shrishant Yadav

A sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete thought. A sentence must have a subject and a predicate. Sentences begin with a capital letter and end with a full stop (.), question mark (?), or exclamation mark (!). In this chapter, you will practice forming, completing, and correcting sentences from basic to advanced level.
