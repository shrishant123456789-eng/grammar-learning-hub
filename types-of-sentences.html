📘 What is a Sentence?

A sentence is a group of words that makes complete sense.

📗 Types of Sentences (Based on Meaning)

1️⃣ Assertive / Declarative Sentence

An Assertive sentence states a fact or opinion.

Rule: It ends with a full stop (.)

Examples:

  • Ram is a good boy.
  • The sun rises in the east.

2️⃣ Interrogative Sentence

An Interrogative sentence asks a question.

Rule: It ends with a question mark (?)

Examples:

  • What is your name?
  • Are you ready?

3️⃣ Imperative Sentence

An Imperative sentence gives an order, advice, request, or command.

Rule: Subject is usually hidden (You).

Examples:

  • Close the door.
  • Please help me.

4️⃣ Exclamatory Sentence

An Exclamatory sentence shows strong feelings or emotions.

Rule: It ends with an exclamation mark (!)

Examples:

  • What a beautiful day!
  • How brave he is!
