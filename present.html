Definition

We use the Simple Present Tense for daily habits, facts, universal truths, and scheduled events.

Hinglish Explanation:
Hum Simple Present ka use tab karte hain jab hum daily habits (roz ki aadat), facts (sachchai), ya regular actions ke baare me baat karte hain. Jaise: "Main school jata hoon" ya "Suraj purab se nikalta hai".

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + V1 (s/es) + Object
Negative: Subject + do/does + not + V1 + Object
Interrogative: Do/Does + Subject + V1 + Object?

Helping Verbs

Do: I, You, We, They, Plural Nouns.
Does: He, She, It, Singular Nouns.

Signal Words

Always, usually, often, sometimes, every day, rarely, never.
(These words tell us the action happens regularly.)

Examples

  • She drinks coffee every morning. (Habit)
  • The sun rises in the east. (Universal Truth)
  • I do not like spicy food. (Fact/Preference)
  • Does he play football? (Question)
  • The train leaves at 6:00 PM. (Schedule)

Common Mistakes

❌ He go to school.
✔ He goes to school.
👉 With He/She/It (Singular), we must add 's' or 'es' to the verb.
❌ I does not know.
✔ I do not know.
👉 'I' takes 'do', not 'does'.
❌ Does she plays tennis?
✔ Does she play tennis?
👉 When 'Does' is used, remove 's/es' from the main verb.

Mini Practice (Simple Present)

Definition

Used to describe actions happening right now at the moment of speaking, or temporary actions happening these days.

Hinglish Explanation:
Iska use tab karte hain jab koi kaam abhi chal raha ho. Jaise: "Main padh raha hoon" (I am reading) ya "Woh khel raha hai" (He is playing).

Structure / Formula

Affirmative: Subject + is/am/are + V1 + ing + Object
Negative: Subject + is/am/are + not + V1 + ing...
Interrogative: Is/Am/Are + Subject + V1 + ing...?

Helping Verbs

Is: He, She, It, Singular.
Am: I (only).
Are: You, We, They, Plural.

Signal Words

Now, right now, at the moment, currently, these days, look!, listen!

Examples

  • I am writing a letter right now.
  • Look! It is raining outside.
  • They are not listening to me.
  • Are you coming with us?
  • She is studying history these days.

Common Mistakes

❌ I am liking this food.
✔ I like this food.
👉 Stative verbs (like, love, know) are usually not used in continuous form.
❌ She is cook dinner.
✔ She is cooking dinner.
👉 Continuous tense always needs 'ing'.
❌ It raining.
✔ It is raining.
👉 Don't forget the helping verb 'is'.

Mini Practice (Present Continuous)

Definition

Used for actions that happened in the past but have a connection to the present, or just finished recently.

Hinglish Explanation:
Jab koi kaam complete ho chuka hai, lekin uska asar abhi bhi hai, ya abhi-abhi khatam hua hai. Isme hum aksar "chuka hai, chuki hai, liya hai" jaise words sochte hain. Example: "Maine khana kha liya hai" (I have eaten).

Structure / Formula

Formula: Subject + has/have + V3 (3rd form) + Object
V3 Example: Go &to; Gone, Eat &to; Eaten, See &to; Seen.

Helping Verbs

Has: He, She, It, Singular.
Have: I, You, We, They, Plural.

Signal Words

Just, already, yet, ever, never, recently, so far, up to now.

Examples

  • I have finished my homework. (Result is important now)
  • She has just left the office.
  • We have not seen that movie yet.
  • Have you ever been to London?
  • He has lost his keys. (He still doesn't have them)

Common Mistakes

❌ I have seen him yesterday.
✔ I saw him yesterday.
👉 If a specific past time (yesterday, last week) is given, use Simple Past, NOT Present Perfect.
❌ She has eat food.
✔ She has eaten food.
👉 Always use V3 (Past Participle) with Has/Have.

Mini Practice (Present Perfect)

Definition

Used for actions that started in the past and are still continuing now.

Hinglish Explanation:
Jab koi kaam pehle shuru hua aur abhi tak chal raha hai. Isme 'time' (kab se) zaroori hota hai. Jaise: "Main subah se padh raha hoon" (I have been reading since morning).

Structure / Formula

Formula: Subject + has/have + been + V1 + ing + (Since/For + Time)

Since vs For

Since: Point of time (Monday, 2010, Morning, 2 PM).
For: Duration (2 hours, 3 days, 5 years, a long time).

Examples

  • I have been waiting for two hours.
  • She has been sleeping since 10 PM.
  • They have been playing cricket all afternoon.
  • It has been raining since last night.

Common Mistakes

❌ I am waiting since 2 hours.
✔ I have been waiting for 2 hours.
👉 When time duration is mentioned with current action, use Perfect Continuous, not Normal Continuous.
❌ She has been working since 5 years.
✔ She has been working for 5 years.
👉 '5 years' is a duration, so use 'for', not 'since'.
