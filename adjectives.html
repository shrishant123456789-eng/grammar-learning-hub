📘 What is an Adjective?

An adjective is a word that describes or qualifies a noun or a pronoun.

a tall boy, a red pen, five apples

📗 Types of Adjectives

1. Adjective of Quality
Shows the kind or quality.
honest man, beautiful girl
2. Adjective of Quantity
Shows amount.
some milk, little sugar
3. Adjective of Number
Shows number or order.
five books, first prize
4. Demonstrative Adjective
Points out nouns.
this pen, those boys
5. Possessive Adjective
Shows possession.
my book, her bag
6. Interrogative Adjective
Used with nouns to ask questions.
which book? whose pen?

📙 Degrees of Comparison

Positive: tall
Comparative: taller
Superlative: tallest
