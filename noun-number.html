Noun: Number

Singular & Plural – Complete Concept with Practice

📘 What is Number?

Number shows whether a noun refers to one person or thing or to more than one.

📗 Kinds of Number

1. Singular Number
A noun that denotes one person, place, animal, or thing.
boy, girl, book, pen
2. Plural Number
A noun that denotes more than one.
boys, girls, books, pens

📙 Rules for Forming Plurals

Rule 1: Add -s to most nouns
boy → boys, pen → pens
Rule 2: Add -es to nouns ending in s, sh, ch, x
box → boxes, bus → buses
Rule 3: Nouns ending in y (after consonant) → change y to ies
city → cities
Rule 4: Some nouns change form
man → men, child → children
Rule 5: Some nouns have the same form
sheep → sheep, deer → deer
