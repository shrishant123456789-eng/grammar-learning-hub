1️⃣ Module Definition: What & Why?

[attachment_0](attachment)
Hinglish: "Tense ka matlab bas itna hai—Time. Is module me hum seekhenge ki kaam abhi ho raha hai (Present), pehle ho chuka hai (Past), ya future me hoga (Future)—aur English me sahi waqt par kaunsa word bolna hai."
English Explanation: Tense tells us when an action happens. If you use the wrong tense, people get confused about the time. This module focuses on the 4 most important tenses for daily conversation.

Why is this important?

  • To tell stories about your yesterday.
  • To talk about your plans for tomorrow.
  • To explain what you are doing right now.
  • To avoid confusion (e.g., saying "I eat" instead of "I ate").

2️⃣ Present Simple (Daily Habits)

Use this for routine, facts, and habits.

"Jo kaam hum roz karte hain, ya jo sach hai. (Ta hai, Ti hai, Te hain). Example: Main office jata hoon."
  • I / You / We / They: Verb (e.g., I work, You go).
  • He / She / It: Verb + s/es (e.g., He works, She goes).

Daily Spoken Examples:

WordDaily SentenceHindi Meaning
Wake upI wake up at 7 AM.Main 7 baje uthta hoon.
WorkShe works very hard.Woh bohot mehnat karti hai.
LikeI like coffee, not tea.Mujhe coffee pasand hai.
LiveWe live in Mumbai.Hum Mumbai me rehte hain.
KnowI know him well.Main use acche se jaanta hoon.

3️⃣ Present Continuous (Happening Now)

Use this for actions happening right now.

"Jo kaam abhi isi waqt chal raha hai. (Raha hai, Rahi hai). Example: Main abhi khaana kha raha hoon."
  • I: am + Verb-ing (I am eating).
  • He / She: is + Verb-ing (He is coming).
  • You / We / They: are + Verb-ing (They are playing).

Daily Spoken Examples:

WordDaily SentenceHindi Meaning
GoI am going to the market.Main market ja raha hoon.
WaitWho are you waiting for?Tum kiska intezaar kar rahe ho?
FeelI am not feeling well today.Meri tabiyat theek nahi lag rahi.
WatchShe is watching TV.Woh TV dekh rahi hai.
KiddingI am just kidding.Main bas mazaak kar raha hoon.

4️⃣ Past Simple (Finished Action)

Use this for actions completed in the past.

"Jo kaam khatam ho gaya. (Gaya, Aaya, Khaya, Kiya). Isme 'is/am/are' nahi lagta, seedha 2nd form lagta hai."
  • Positive: Subject + V2 (I went, She cooked).
  • Negative: Didn't + V1 (I didn't go).

Daily Spoken Examples:

WordDaily SentenceHindi Meaning
CallHe called me yesterday.Usne mujhe kal call kiya.
ForgetSorry, I forgot your name.Sorry, main tumhara naam bhool gaya.
MeetI met him at the cafe.Main use cafe me mila.
SleepI slept late last night.Main kal raat der se soya.
BuyWe bought a new car.Humne nayi car khareedi.

5️⃣ Future (Will / Going to)

Use for plans and future predictions.

"Jo kaam future me hoga. 'Will' formal ya instant decision ke liye. 'Going to' pakke plan ke liye."
  • Will: I will call you later. (Instant decision)
  • Going to: I am going to buy a laptop. (Planned)

Daily Spoken Examples:

WordDaily SentenceHindi Meaning
HelpI will help you.Main tumhari madad karunga.
ComeHe will come tomorrow.Woh kal aayega.
StartI am going to start a business.Main business shuru karne wala hoon.
CheckI will check and tell you.Main check karke bataunga.

6️⃣ Signal Words & Time Clues

These words tell you which tense to use instantly.

🕰️ For Present Simple (Routine)

Every day, Usually, Often, Sometimes, Always, Never.

⚡ For Present Continuous (Now)

Now, Right now, At the moment, Look!, Listen!, Currently.

🔙 For Past Simple (History)

Yesterday, Last night, Last week, In 2010, 2 days ago, This morning (if morning is over).

🔮 For Future

Tomorrow, Next week, Tonight, Soon, Later.

7️⃣ Spoken Sentence Patterns

Memorize these structures for fluency.

Pattern 1: Routine Question
"Do you...?"
👉 Do you exercise daily?
👉 Do you like spicy food?
Pattern 2: Past Question
"Did you...?"
👉 Did you call him?
👉 Did you finish the work?
Pattern 3: Ongoing Activity
"Are you...?"
👉 Are you listening to me?
👉 Are you coming with us?

8️⃣ Common Indian Mistakes

❌ Wrong (Don't Say) ✅ Correct (Say This) Reason
I am go to office everyday. I go to office everyday. Routine me 'am' nahi lagta.
I didn't went there. I didn't go there. Didn't ke saath hamesha 1st form.
She cooking food. She is cooking food. Helping verb (is) zaroori hai.
Yesterday I sleep late. Yesterday I slept late. Beeti baat ke liye 2nd form.
