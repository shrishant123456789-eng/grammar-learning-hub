📖 What is this Module?

English Basics is the first step for anyone who wants to speak English fluently but doesn't know where to start. This module focuses purely on Spoken English, not boring grammar rules.

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation:

“Is module me hum English ke bilkul basic concepts seekhenge. Agar aapko lagta hai ki English bolna bohot mushkil hai, toh ye module aapka darr khatam karega. Hum seekhenge ki words ko kaise bolna hai, sentences kaise banana hai (S + V + O rule), aur daily life me use hone wale sawal kaise poochne hain.”

🇬🇧 English Explanation:

In this module, we will learn the building blocks of English. You will learn correct pronunciation, essential daily words, and the secret formula to make any sentence. By the end of this module, you will be able to speak simple sentences confidently.

⚡ Key Takeaways:
  • Alphabet Sounds: Understanding that letters have different sounds (Phonics).
  • Vowels vs Consonants: The glue that holds words together.
  • S + V + O Rule: The golden rule for 90% of English sentences.
  • Question Formation: How to ask 'Yes/No' and 'Wh-' questions easily.
  • Confidence: Breaking the fear of making mistakes.

📚 Select a Topic to Learn

▶ Topic 1: Introduction to English Basics

Welcome! Before we speak, we must understand that English is just a language, not a measure of intelligence.

Sabse pehle ye samjhein: English sirf ek language hai, communication ka tool hai. Galti karne se mat dariye. Bachcha bhi gir-gir kar chalna seekhta hai.

Goal of Module 1: To make you comfortable with the structure of English.

▶ Topic 2: Alphabet & Sounds (A–Z)

English has 26 letters, but 44 sounds. Writing is different from Speaking.

  • A - Z: The written letters.
  • Phonics: The sound they make inside a word.

Example: Letter 'C' ka naam 'See' hai, par sound 'Ka' (Cat) ya 'Sa' (City) hota hai. Hum sounds par focus karenge.

Practice: Say these aloud clearly: Apple, Ball, Cat, Dog, Elephant.

▶ Topic 3: Vowels & Consonants

Vowels (Swara): A, E, I, O, U. These give sound to words.

Consonants (Vyanjan): All other letters (B, C, D, F...).

Bina Vowel ke English ka koi word nahi banta (exception: rhythm/sky jahan 'y' vowel ka kaam karta hai). Vowels hi word ko "awaaz" dete hain.

▶ Topic 4: Common Daily Words

To start speaking, you need a "Toolkit" of words.

Subject (Doer) Action (Verb) Object/Place
I (Main) Go (Jaana) Market
You (Tum/Aap) Eat (Khaana) Food
We (Hum) Play (Khelna) Cricket
They (Ve log) Speak (Bolna) English
▶ Topic 5: Sentence Order (S + V + O)

This is the Golden Rule of English.

Subject + Verb + Object

(Who is doing?) + (What action?) + (To whom/what?)

Hindi: Main (S) cricket (O) khelta hoon (V). (S-O-V)
English: I (S) play (V) cricket (O). (S-V-O)

Note: Hindi me verb last me aata hai, English me Subject ke turant baad aata hai.

Examples:
✅ I love music.
✅ She drinks water.
✅ We learn English.

▶ Topic 6: Yes / No Questions

Questions that can be answered with just "Yes" or "No".

Formula: Helping Verb + Subject + Main Verb?

  • Do you like tea? (Kya apko chai pasand hai?)
  • Are you happy? (Kya aap khush hain?)
  • Can you swim? (Kya aap tair sakte hain?)
▶ Topic 7: Wh-Questions

Used to ask for information. They cannot be answered with Yes/No.

  • What: Kya (Things) -> What is your name?
  • Where: Kahan (Place) -> Where do you live?
  • When: Kab (Time) -> When will you come?
  • Why: Kyun (Reason) -> Why are you sad?
  • Who: Kaun (Person) -> Who is he?
  • How: Kaise (Process) -> How are you?
▶ Topic 8: Common Beginner Mistakes
❌ Wrong (Common Indian Mistakes) ✅ Correct Spoken English
I am go to market. I am going to market.
He have a car. He has a car.
I am agree. I agree.
She don't know. She doesn't know.
▶ Topic 9: Confidence Tips

1. Translation band karo: Mann me Hindi se English translate mat karo. Direct English words socho.

2. Mirror Practice: Sheeshe ke samne khade hokar khud se baat karo.

3. Speak Loudly: Dhire bologe to darr lagega. Tez aawaz me confidence aata hai.

Remember: Mistake is proof that you are trying.
