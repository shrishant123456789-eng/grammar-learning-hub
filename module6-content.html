1️⃣ Module Definition: Sentence Structure

"Hum aksar words toh sahi bolte hain, lekin unka order galat ho jata hai. Ya fir hum 'I go' bolkar chup ho jate hain. Is module me hum seekhenge ki ek chhota sentence kaise bada aur complete banta hai."

What is Sentence Structure?

Sentence structure is the order of words. The most basic rule in English is SVO:

S (Subject) + V (Verb) + O (Object)

✅ I (Subject) play (Verb) cricket (Object).
❌ I cricket play. (This is Hindi structure)

Native speakers sound fluent because they don't just use SVO; they expand it with time, place, and reason.

2️⃣ Expanding Short Sentences

Imagine a sentence is like a train. The engine is "Subject + Verb". But you need wagons to carry passengers (details).

The Expansion Formula:

  • Level 1 (Basic): I drink coffee.
  • Level 2 (+ Place): I drink coffee in the office.
  • Level 3 (+ Time): I drink coffee in the office every morning.
  • Level 4 (+ Reason): I drink coffee in the office every morning to stay active.

See? You just moved from broken English to fluent English!

4️⃣ Adding Time & Place Naturally

"Confusion hota hai ki 'Yesterday' pehle lagayein ya baad me? 'At home' kahan aayega?"

Rule: Place before Time (PT Rule)

Usually, we say WHERE first, then WHEN.

Example: I went to Mumbai (Place) last week (Time).
❌ I went last week to Mumbai. (Less natural)

Time Emphasis

You can put Time at the very beginning to emphasize it.

  • ✅ Yesterday, I met him. (Focus on Yesterday)
  • ✅ I met him yesterday. (Neutral)

5️⃣ Short vs Natural Spoken Sentences

Compare these sentences. The expanded ones show confidence.

We played.We played cricket in the park yesterday.

6️⃣ Hindi Thinking vs English Structure

"Hindi me verb last me aata hai. English me verb Subject ke turant baad aata hai."
Hindi: Main (Subject) aam (Object) khaata hoon (Verb).
English: I (Subject) eat (Verb) a mango (Object).

Common Word Order Mistakes

  • ❌ I car drive. -> ✅ I drive a car.
  • ❌ She fast runs. -> ✅ She runs fast. (Adverb comes after verb)
  • ❌ I very much like it. -> ✅ I like it very much.

7️⃣ Daily Life Sentence Expansion

Let's take real situations and build sentences.

Scenario: Office

Short: I work.

Expanded: I work as a developer in an MNC in Pune.

Scenario: Weekend

Short: I slept.

Expanded: I slept late on Sunday because it was a holiday.

Scenario: Food

Short: Food is good.

Expanded: This biryani is very delicious and spicy.

8️⃣ Common Sentence Structure Mistakes

Wrong SentenceCorrect SentenceReason
I like very much cricket.I like cricket very much.Object (cricket) comes after Verb (like).
I yesterday went to park.I went to the park yesterday.Time usually goes at the end.
Because I was ill so I didn't come.Because I was ill, I didn't come.Don't use 'Because' and 'So' together.
She to the market went.She went to the market.Subject + Verb + Place.
