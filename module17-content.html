🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation: Darr Kyun Lagta Hai?

Sach baat ye hai: Hamein English se darr nahi lagta, hamein "Judgement" se darr lagta hai. "Log kya sochenge agar maine galti ki?" - Ye soch hamein chup kara deti hai.

Public speaking ka matlab ye nahi hai ki aap Shakespeare ban jao. Iska matlab hai: Apni baat ko clarity ke saath rakhna.

Jab aap stage par hote ho, log aapki grammar check nahi kar rahe hote, wo aapka CONFIDENCE aur MESSAGE sun rahe hote hain.

🇬🇧 Theory: The 3 Pillars of Speaking

  • Clarity: Speak slowly. Don't rush. A pause is powerful, not awkward.
  • Structure: Start with a hook, give your main point, and end with a summary.
  • Authenticity: Don't memorize lines. Speak like you are talking to a friend, just louder.
