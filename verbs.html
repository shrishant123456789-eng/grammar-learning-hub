Definition

A verb is a word used to describe an action, state, or occurrence.

Simplest Definition: Verb wo shabd hai jo kisi kaam ka hona (Action), sthiti (State), ya adhikar (Possession) batata hai.
Examples:
  • She plays cricket. (Action - Khelna)
  • He is a doctor. (State - Hona)
  • I have a car. (Possession - Pass hona)

The "Magic Questions" Trick

To find the verb in any sentence, ask these questions:

Ask: "Kya kar raha hai?" (Action)
Ask: "Kya hai?" (State)
Sentence Question (Mann me socho) Answer (Verb)
Rahul runs fast. Rahul kya kar raha hai? Runs (Daud raha hai)
She is sad. Wo kaisi hai? (State) Is (Hai)
They eat mangoes. Wo kya kar rahe hai? Eat (Kha rahe hai)
I have a pen. Mere pass kya hai? Have (Pass hai)
Birds fly. Birds kya karti hai? Fly (Udti hai)

Verbs are mainly divided into two categories:

A. Main Verb (Mukhya Kriya)

Shows the actual action.

Jo shabd asli kaam bataye. Example: Go, Sleep, Eat, Dance.

B. Auxiliary / Helping Verb (Sahayak Kriya)

Helps the main verb to form tenses or mood.

Ye main verb ki madad karta hai time batane me. Example: Is, am, are, was, were, has, have.
She is (Helping) playing (Main).

C. Modal Verbs

Show possibility, ability, or permission.

List: Can, Could, May, Might, Should, Must.

Ex: I can swim. (Main tair sakta hu - Ability).

D. Transitive vs Intransitive

Transitive (Sakarmak) Intransitive (Akarmak)
Needs an Object to complete meaning. Does NOT need an object.
He kicked the ball.
(Kicked WHAT? Ball. Object hai.)		 He slept.
(Slept WHAT? No answer. Complete meaning.)

Every Main Verb has 5 forms used in different tenses.

V1 (Base) V2 (Past) V3 (Perfect) V4 (Ing) V5 (s/es)
Go Went Gone Going Goes
Eat Ate Eaten Eating Eats
Play Played Played Playing Plays
Cut Cut Cut Cutting Cuts
Note: V1 aur V5 Present Tense me use hote hai. V2 Past me. V3 Perfect tenses me.

Which verb form goes with which tense?

1. Present Tense

  • Simple: V1 or V5 (He plays, I play)
  • Continuous: Is/Am/Are + V4 (He is playing)
  • Perfect: Has/Have + V3 (He has played)

2. Past Tense

  • Simple: V2 (He played)
  • Continuous: Was/Were + V4 (He was playing)
  • Perfect: Had + V3 (He had played)

3. Future Tense

  • Simple: Will/Shall + V1 (He will play)
  • Continuous: Will be + V4 (He will be playing)

Don't make these beginner mistakes!

❌ Wrong ✔ Correct Why?
He go to school. He goes to school. Singular subject (He) needs V5 (s/es).
Did she went there? Did she go there? With 'Did', always use V1.
I can to swim. I can swim. No 'to' after Modals (can, may, etc).
She has eat food. She has eaten food. With Has/Have, use V3.
He is play. He is playing. Is/Am/Are needs 'ing' form (V4).
