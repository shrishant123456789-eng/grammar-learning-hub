① WHAT IS A PRONOUN?

📚 Definition

A pronoun is a word that replaces a noun to avoid repetition and make sentences smoother and clearer.

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

Pronoun wo shabd hota hai jo noun ki jagah use hota hai taaki baar-baar noun repeat na karna pade. Jaise 'Rahul' ki jagah 'he'.

🧮 Formula

Pronoun = Replaces Noun

✅ Correct Examples

Rahul is my friend. He is very kind.

I saw Sita. She was reading.

This is my book. It is new.

I met Rahul and Sita. They are nice.

This pen is mine.

❌ Wrong Examples

✗ Rahul is my friend. Rahul is very kind. (Repetition - boring!)

✗ I saw Sita. Sita was reading. (Repetition - not smooth)

✗ This is my book. My book is new. (Too repetitive)

③ PERSONAL PRONOUNS

📖 Definition

Personal pronouns are pronouns that refer to specific persons or things. They change form according to person, number, and case.

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

Personal pronouns wo hote hain jo kisi specific insaan ya cheez ki baat karte hain. Ye pronouns person, number aur case ke hisaab se change hote hain.

✅ Key Rules

  • Subject Pronouns: I, you, he, she, it, we, they (karta/कर्ता के लिए)
  • Object Pronouns: me, you, him, her, it, us, them (karma/कर्म के लिए)
  • First Person: I, me, we, us (bolne wala)
  • Second Person: you (sunte wale ke लिए)
  • Third Person: he, she, it, they, him, her, them (teesra vyakti)
Person Subject Pronoun Object Pronoun Example
1st Person Singular I me I am happy. They saw me.
2nd Person Singular you you You are kind. I like you.
3rd Person Singular (Male) he him He is tall. She called him.
3rd Person Singular (Female) she her She is smart. I met her.
3rd Person Singular (Neutral) it it It is cold. I like it.
1st Person Plural we us We are friends. They invited us.
3rd Person Plural they them They are coming. I saw them.

❌ Common Mistakes

Wrong: Me am happy. (Object pronoun as subject)

Right: I am happy.

Why wrong: Subject ke liye hamesha subject pronoun (I, you, he, she, it, we, they) use karte hain!

Wrong: Between you and I (preposition ke baad subject pronoun)

Right: Between you and me

Why wrong: Preposition (between, to, for, with) ke baad hamesha object pronoun (me, you, him, her, us, them) aata hai!

SSC TIP: Subject pronoun = karta (I, he, she, they), Object pronoun = karm (me, him, her, them)!

④ POSSESSIVE PRONOUNS

📖 Definition

Possessive pronouns show ownership or possession. They indicate who owns what.

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

Possessive pronouns ownership ya kabza dikhate hain. Ye batate hain ki kisi cheez ka malik kaun hai.

✅ Key Rules

  • Possessive Adjectives: my, your, his, her, its, our, their (noun ke pehle aate hain)
  • Possessive Pronouns: mine, yours, his, hers, its, ours, theirs (khud hi noun ka kaam karte hain)
  • Its vs It's: "Its" = possessive, "It's" = it is
  • His - dono adjective aur pronoun ka kaam karta hai

✅ Possessive Adjectives (Before Noun)

This is my book.

That is your pen.

His car is new.

Her dress is beautiful.

Their house is big.

✅ Possessive Pronouns (Replace Noun)

This book is mine.

That pen is yours.

The car is his.

The dress is hers.

The house is theirs.

❌ Common Mistakes

Wrong: This book is my. (Adjective khud stand nahin kar sakta)

Right: This book is mine.

Why wrong: Jab pronoun khud stand kare to 'mine' use karte hain, 'my' nahin!

Wrong: Its a nice day. (Confusion between its and it's)

Right: It's a nice day.

Why wrong: "It's" = "It is", "Its" = possessive form!

Wrong: This is yours book. ('s' double ho gaya)

Right: This is your book. OR This book is yours.

Why wrong: 'Yours' mein already possessive meaning hai, noun ke pehle 'your' use karte hain!

SSC TIP: Agar noun hai sentence mein to 'your', agar noun nahin hai to 'yours'!

⑤ REFLEXIVE & EMPHATIC PRONOUNS

📖 Definition

Reflexive Pronouns: When the subject and object are the same person/thing.

Emphatic Pronouns: Same words but used for emphasis.

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

Reflexive pronouns tab use hote hain jab karta aur karma ek hi ho. Emphatic pronouns same words hote hain lekin stress dene ke liye.

✅ Key Rules

  • Myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves
  • Reflexive: I hurt myself. (Subject = Object)
  • Emphatic: I myself did it. (Stress ke liye)
  • Kabhi bhi reflexive pronoun subject nahin ban sakta!

✅ Reflexive Pronouns

I hurt myself.

She looked at herself in the mirror.

He blamed himself.

We enjoyed ourselves.

They helped themselves.

✅ Emphatic Pronouns

I myself did the work.

She herself cooked the food.

He himself admitted the mistake.

We ourselves saw the incident.

They themselves admitted it.

❌ Common Mistakes

Wrong: Myself will do it. (Reflexive as subject)

Right: I myself will do it. OR I will do it myself.

Why wrong: Reflexive pronoun kabhi bhi subject nahin ban sakta!

Wrong: I and Rahul enjoyed ourselves. (Mixing subjects)

Right: Rahul and I enjoyed ourselves.

Why wrong: Doosra subject (Rahul) hone par reflexive pronoun plural hota hai!

Wrong: Please yourself. (Wrong context)

Right: Help yourself. (Correct reflexive use)

Why wrong: 'Please yourself' alag meaning hai - context matters!

SSC TIP: Agar sentence mein "by" ho to generally reflexive pronoun aata hai: "She did it by herself"!

⑥ DEMONSTRATIVE PRONOUNS

📖 Definition

Demonstrative pronouns point to specific things and show their distance from the speaker.

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

Demonstrative pronouns specific cheezon ki taraf point karte hain aur dikhate hain ki wo speaker ke kitne paas ya door hain.

✅ Key Rules

  • This: Near, singular (ye - paas, ek)
  • That: Far, singular (wo - door, ek)
  • These: Near, plural (ye - paas, bahut)
  • Those: Far, plural (wo - door, bahut)
  • Distance physical ya time dono ho sakti hai!

✅ Distance Examples

This is my book. (Paas mein)

That is your house. (Door mein)

These are my friends. (Paas mein, bahut)

Those are mountains. (Door mein, bahut)

✅ Time Examples

This week is busy. (Current/near time)

That day was memorable. (Past/far time)

These days are hard. (Current days)

Those were the days! (Past days)

❌ Common Mistakes

Wrong: This books are mine. (Singular pronoun with plural noun)

Right: These books are mine.

Why wrong: 'This' singular hai, 'books' plural - match hona chahiye!

Wrong: Those is my friend. (Plural pronoun with singular noun)

Right: That is my friend.

Why wrong: 'Those' plural hai, 'friend' singular - number match karna important hai!

Wrong: This are my shoes. (Wrong verb)

Right: These are my shoes.

Why wrong: Pronoun aur verb dono ka number match hona chahiye!

SSC TIP: This/That = singular, These/Those = plural. Aur hamesha noun ka number dekhkar pronoun chuno!

⑦ RELATIVE PRONOUNS

📖 Definition

Relative pronouns connect a clause or phrase to a noun or pronoun. They introduce relative clauses.

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

Relative pronouns do sentences ko connect karte hain. Ye relative clauses introduce karte hain.

✅ Key Rules

  • Who: People ke liye (subject)
  • Whom: People ke liye (object)
  • Which: Things/animals ke liye
  • That: People aur things dono ke liye
  • Whose: Possession dikhane ke liye
  • Where: Place ke liye
  • When: Time ke liye

✅ Who/Whom Examples

The man who came yesterday is my uncle.

The girl whom I met is very nice.

The boy whose book this is must come forward.

✅ Which/That Examples

The book which you gave me is interesting.

The pen that I bought is expensive.

The house where I live is big.

The day when we met was special.

❌ Common Mistakes

Wrong: The man which came yesterday... (Wrong pronoun for person)

Right: The man who came yesterday...

Why wrong: People ke liye hamesha 'who' ya 'that', 'which' nahin!

Wrong: The book who you gave me... (Wrong pronoun for thing)

Right: The book which/that you gave me...

Why wrong: Things ke liye 'which' ya 'that' use karte hain, 'who' nahin!

Wrong: Who did you meet? (Formal writing mein)

Right: Whom did you meet? (Formal)

Why: Preposition ya verb ke object ke liye 'whom' use karte hain!

SSC TIP: Who = subject (karta), Whom = object (karma). Agar 'he' fit kare to who, agar 'him' fit kare to whom!

⑧ INTERROGATIVE PRONOUNS

📖 Definition

Interrogative pronouns are used to ask questions. They help us get information about people, things, or ideas.

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

Interrogative pronouns sawal puchne ke liye use hote hain. Ye hume people, things ya ideas ke baare mein information lene mein help karte hain.

✅ Key Rules

  • Who: People ke baare mein sawal (subject)
  • Whom: People ke baare mein sawal (object)
  • What: Things/ideas ke baare mein sawal
  • Which: Choice ke baare mein sawal
  • Whose: Ownership ke baare mein sawal

✅ Question Examples

Who is coming to the party?

Whom did you meet yesterday?

What is your name?

Which book do you want?

Whose pen is this?

✅ Answer Examples

Who won the match? - Rahul did.

What happened? - Nothing special.

Which is better? - This one is.

Whose turn is it? - It's mine.

❌ Common Mistakes

Wrong: Who book is this? (Wrong pronoun for thing)

Right: Whose book is this? (Ownership) OR What book is this? (Type)

Why wrong: 'Who' sirf people ke liye hota hai, things ke liye 'what' ya 'whose'!

Wrong: What did come to the party? (Wrong pronoun for person)

Right: Who came to the party?

Why wrong: People ke liye 'who' use karte hain, 'what' nahin!

Wrong: Who did you give the book? (Missing preposition)

Right: Who did you give the book to? OR Whom did you give the book?

Why: Preposition ke object ke liye 'whom' ya 'to whom' use karte hain!

SSC TIP: Who/Whom = people, What = things/ideas, Which = choice, Whose = ownership!

⑨ INDEFINITE PRONOUNS

📖 Definition

Indefinite pronouns refer to non-specific people or things. They don't point to any particular person or thing.

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

Indefinite pronouns non-specific logon ya cheezon ki baat karte hain. Ye kisi particular person ya thing ki taraf point nahin karte.

✅ Key Rules

  • Some: Positive sentences mein (kuch)
  • Any: Negative aur questions mein (koi bhi)
  • None: Koi nahin
  • All: Sab
  • Each/Every: Har ek (singular verb)
  • Everyone/Someone/Anyone: Har koi/koi/koi bhi

✅ Positive Examples

Some people are coming.

All students passed.

Each person has a ticket.

Everyone is happy.

Someone is at the door.

✅ Negative/Question Examples

I don't have any money.

Is anyone there?

None of them came.

Do you need anything?

Nobody knows the answer.

❌ Common Mistakes

Wrong: Everyone are happy. (Wrong verb)

Right: Everyone is happy.

Why wrong: Everyone, someone, anyone, each - ye sab singular hote hain, plural verb nahin!

Wrong: Each students have a book. (Wrong verb form)

Right: Each student has a book.

Why wrong: Each ke baat singular noun aur singular verb aata hai!

Wrong: I don't have some money. (Wrong in negative)

Right: I don't have any money.

Why: Negative sentences mein 'any' use karte hain, 'some' nahin!

SSC TIP: Each/Every/Everyone/Someone = singular verb (is, has, does). Some = positive, Any = negative/questions!

⑩ COMMON ERRORS & EXAM TRAPS

❌ Wrong ✔ Correct Why Wrong?
Me and Rahul went Rahul and I went Subject ke liye 'I' use karte hain, 'me' nahin
Between you and I Between you and me Preposition ke baad object pronoun 'me' aata hai
Each students are Each student is Each singular hai - singular noun aur verb lagta hai
This books are These books are This singular, books plural - number match karna hai
Who did you meet to? Who did you meet? OR Whom did you meet? Preposition ke object ke liye 'whom' use karte hain
The man which came The man who came People ke liye 'who', things ke liye 'which'
Myself will do it I myself will do it Reflexive pronoun kabhi subject nahin ban sakta
This is yours book This is your book OR This book is yours 'Yours' mein already possessive meaning hai - double possession wrong
SSC EXAM TRAP: Subject/Object confusion, singular/plural mismatch, who/whom confusion - ye top 3 mistakes hain!
