📘 What is a Pronoun?

A pronoun is a word used in place of a noun to avoid repetition.

📗 Types of Pronouns

1. Personal Pronoun
Used to refer to persons or things.
I, we, you, he, she, it, they
2. Relative Pronoun
Used to join clauses.
who, whom, whose, which, that
3. Demonstrative Pronoun
Points out specific persons or things.
this, that, these, those
4. Indefinite Pronoun
Refers to persons or things in a general way.
someone, anyone, everybody
5. Reflexive Pronoun
Refers back to the subject.
myself, himself, herself
6. Emphatic Pronoun
Used to emphasize the subject.
I myself did it
