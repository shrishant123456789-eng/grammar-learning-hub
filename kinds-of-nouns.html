📘 What is a Noun?

A noun is a word that names a person, place, thing, or idea.

📗 Kinds of Nouns

1. Proper Noun
A Proper Noun is the name of a particular person, place, or thing.
Ram, Delhi, India, Ganga
2. Common Noun
A Common Noun is a general name of a person, place, or thing.
boy, city, river, book
3. Collective Noun
A Collective Noun denotes a group or collection.
team, class, army, bunch
4. Abstract Noun
An Abstract Noun denotes a quality, state, or idea.
honesty, courage, childhood
5. Material Noun
A Material Noun denotes a substance or material.
gold, water, milk, iron
