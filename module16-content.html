The Power of Advanced Vocabulary

🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation

Advanced Vocabulary ka matlab "mushkil words" (difficult words) nahi hota. Iska matlab hai sahi jagah par sahi word use karna.

Agar aap office me "I want to talk to you" bologe toh wo rude lag sakta hai. Lekin agar aap "I'd like to have a word with you" bologe, toh wo professional aur polite lagega.

Is module me hum seekhenge ki kaise Idioms, Phrasal Verbs, aur Collocations use karke hum apni English ko natural aur confident bana sakte hain.

🇬🇧 Theory: What makes English "Natural"?

  • Idioms: Phrases like "Piece of cake" express emotion better than "Easy".
  • Phrasal Verbs: Native speakers say "Give up" instead of "Quit".
  • Collocations: Words that naturally go together, like "Heavy rain" (not "Strong rain").
