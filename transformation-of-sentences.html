Transformation of Sentences

Master Sentence Interchanges without Changing Meaning

1. Introduction

Definition: Transformation of sentences means changing the form (structure) of a sentence without changing its meaning (sense).
Sentence ka roop badalna hai, par uska matlab wahi rehna chahiye.
Example: "He is a good boy." -> "He is not a bad boy." (Meaning same hai).

Why is it important?

It tests your ability to express the same idea in multiple ways. This is crucial for SSC, Banking, and Board exams.

2️⃣ Assertive ↔ Interrogative

Rule 1: If the sentence is affirmative, the question must be negative.
Rule 2: If the sentence is negative, the question must be affirmative.

  • Assertive: He is a great man.
  • Interrogative: Is he not a great man?
  • Assertive: Everybody loves his country.
  • Interrogative: Who does not love his country?
  • Assertive: No one can touch the sky.
  • Interrogative: Who can touch the sky?
3️⃣ Assertive ↔ Imperative

Imperative sentences express order, request, or advice. Usually, the subject 'You' is hidden.

Agar sentence "You should" se shuru ho, toh bas Verb se start karo. Agar 1st/3rd person ho toh "Let" use karo.
  • Assertive: You should do the work.
  • Imperative: Do the work.
  • Assertive: You are requested to help me.
  • Imperative: Please help me.
  • Assertive: We should play football.
  • Imperative: Let us play football.
4️⃣ Affirmative ↔ Negative

Use antonyms with 'not' to keep the meaning the same. Also use phrases like "None but", "No sooner... than".

  • Aff: Man is mortal.
  • Neg: Man is not immortal.
  • Aff: Only God can help us.
  • Neg: None but God can help us.
  • Aff: As soon as he came, it rained.
  • Neg: No sooner had he come than it rained.
5️⃣ Active ↔ Passive Voice

Swap Subject and Object. Always use Verb 3rd form (V3). Use 'by' (mostly).

  • Active: I write a letter.
  • Passive: A letter is written by me.
  • Active: Who broke the glass?
  • Passive: By whom was the glass broken?
6️⃣ Degrees of Comparison
Positive: No other... as good as...
Comparative: Better than any other...
Superlative: The best...
  • Sup: Iron is the most useful metal.
  • Comp: Iron is more useful than any other metal.
  • Pos: No other metal is as useful as iron.
7️⃣ Exclamatory ↔ Assertive

Change "What/How" to "Very" or "Great". Remove exclamation mark (!).

  • Excl: What a beautiful flower it is!
  • Ass: It is a very beautiful flower.
  • Excl: O that I were a bird!
  • Ass: I wish that I were a bird.
8️⃣ Simple ↔ Compound ↔ Complex

Simple: One Subject + One Finite Verb.
Compound: Connected by FANBOYS (For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So).
Complex: Connected by subordinators (If, Because, Though, When, That).

  • Simple: In spite of being poor, he is honest.
  • Compound: He is poor, but he is honest.
  • Complex: Though he is poor, he is honest.
9️⃣ Direct ↔ Indirect Speech

Change tense to past (mostly), change pronouns, change time words (now -> then).

  • Direct: He said, "I am busy."
  • Indirect: He said that he was busy.
🔟 Interchange of Parts of Speech

Changing the word class (Noun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb) without changing meaning.

  • Verb to Noun: He admitted his guilt. → He made an admission of his guilt.
  • Adverb to Adjective: He fought bravely. → He put up a brave fight.

11. SSC & Competitive Exam Tricks 🚀

  • Elimination Method: First check if the meaning has changed. If yes, eliminate that option immediately.
  • Tense Check: In transformation (unlike Direct/Indirect), Tense usually does NOT change (except Active/Passive nuances). If question is Present, answer usually remains Present.
  • Too...to: Always converts to "So...that...cannot/could not".
  • No sooner: Always followed by 'than'. (Hardware rule: No sooner + did/had + Subject).
