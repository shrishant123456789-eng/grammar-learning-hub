📘 What is a Noun?

A Noun is a word used as the name of a person, place, animal, thing, or idea.

Examples:

  • Person: Ram, Sita, Teacher
  • Place: Delhi, School, India
  • Thing: Book, Table, Phone
  • Animal: Dog, Cow, Lion
  • Idea: Honesty, Love, Happiness

📗 Kinds of Nouns

1. Proper Noun: Special name of a person or place.
Example: Ram, Delhi

2. Common Noun: General name of a person or place.
Example: boy, city

3. Collective Noun: Name of a group.
Example: team, class

4. Abstract Noun: Name of a quality or idea.
Example: honesty, bravery

5. Material Noun: Name of material or substance.
Example: gold, water
