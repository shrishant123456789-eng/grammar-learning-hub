Chapter 1: Noun

Basic to Advanced Practice

Introduction

A noun is the name of a person, place, animal, thing, or idea.

Rules

  • Names of people are nouns
  • Names of places are nouns
  • Names of things are nouns

Practice Questions

1. Identify the noun in the sentence:
The boy is playing.

2. Which one is a noun?
run, blue, honesty, quickly
