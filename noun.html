The Noun

Master Naming Words: Types, Numbers, Gender & Cases

1. Introduction to Nouns

Definition: A Noun is the name of a person, place, animal, thing, or idea.
Noun matlab 'Sangya'. Kisi bhi vyakti, jagah, cheez, ya bhavna ke naam ko Noun kehte hain. Asaan bhasha mein: "Naming Words".

Examples:

  • Person: Ram, Girl, Doctor
  • Place: Delhi, School, India
  • Thing: Table, Phone, Gold
  • Idea/Feeling: Love, Honesty, Freedom

2. Kinds of Nouns

1️⃣ Proper Noun (Vyakti-vachak Sangya)

Specific name of a particular person, place, or thing. Always starts with a Capital Letter.

Kisi khaas (specific) naam ko Proper Noun kehte hain. Jaise 'City' common hai, par 'Mumbai' proper hai.
  • Examples: Virat Kohli, The Taj Mahal, Sunday, July.
Wrong ❌Correct ✔Reason
i live in india.I live in India.Proper nouns start with Capital letters.

Practice: Proper Noun

1. The capital of France is ____.
2️⃣ Common Noun (Jati-vachak Sangya)

A name given in common to every person or thing of the same class or kind.

Jo naam sabke liye use ho sake. Jaise 'Ladka' (Boy) kisi bhi ladke ko bol sakte hain.
  • Examples: Boy, City, Country, Book, Teacher.
3️⃣ Collective Noun (Samuh-vachak Sangya)

Name of a group or collection of persons or things taken together.

Poore group ka ek naam. Ye usually Singular verb leta hai.
  • A team of players.
  • A bunch of keys.
  • An army of soldiers.
  • A fleet of ships.

Practice: Collective Noun

1. A group of judges is called a ____.
4️⃣ Material Noun (Padarth-vachak Sangya)

Name of matter or substance of which things are made. They are Uncountable.

Jis cheez se doosri cheez banti hai. Iske pehle 'A/An' nahi lagta.
  • Examples: Gold, Silver, Wood, Milk, Iron.
Wrong ❌Correct ✔Reason
The table is made of woods.The table is made of wood.Material nouns are not pluralized (Woods = jungle).
5️⃣ Abstract Noun (Bhav-vachak Sangya)

Name of a quality, action, or state. You cannot touch or see it, only feel it.

Wo cheezein jo dikhayi nahi deti bas mehsoos hoti hain. Jaise imandari, bachpan.
  • Quality: Goodness, Honesty, Bravery.
  • State: Childhood, Youth, Poverty.
  • Action: Laughter, Theft.

3. The Noun: Number

Singular & Plural Rules

Basic Rule: Add 's' (Boy -> Boys).

Important Rules for Exams:

1. Ends in s, sh, ch, x: Add 'es' (Box -> Boxes, Bench -> Benches).

2. Ends in y (consonant before y): Change 'y' to 'ies' (City -> Cities).

3. Ends in f/fe: Change to 'ves' (Wife -> Wives, Knife -> Knives).

Exception: Roof -> Roofs, Chief -> Chiefs.

4. Compound Noun: Main word changes (Brother-in-law -> Brothers-in-law).

5. Latin/Greek:
• Datum -> Data
• Radius -> Radii
• Phenomenon -> Phenomena

Practice: Numbers

1. The plural of 'Child' is ____.
2. The plural of 'Sheep' is ____.

4. The Noun: Gender

  • Masculine: Male (Boy, King, Lion).
  • Feminine: Female (Girl, Queen, Lioness).
  • Common: Either male or female (Teacher, Student, Doctor).
  • Neuter: Non-living things (Table, Pen, Room).

5. The Noun: Case (Apostrophe)

Possessive Case Rules

Used to show ownership/possession.

Haq ya adhikaar dikhane ke liye 's use hota hai. (Ram's book = Ram ki kitab).

Rules:

  1. Living things: Use 's (Ram's cap).
  2. Non-living: Use 'of' (Leg of table ❌ Table's leg).
  3. Plural ending in S: Use only apostrophe ' (Boys' hostel).
  4. Plural not ending in S: Use 's (Men's club).

Practice: Case

1. Convert: "School for boys" -> The ____.

⚠️ Most Common Exam Errors

These nouns are frequently asked in SSC/Competitive exams.

1. Uncountable Nouns (Always Singular Verb)

These nouns do not have a plural form. Do not add 's'.

Scenery, Furniture, Advice, Information, Luggage, Baggage, Poetry, Hair, Mischief.
Wrong ❌Correct ✔
He gave me informations.He gave me information.
My hairs are black.My hair is black.
The sceneries of Kashmir.The scenery of Kashmir.

2. Plural in appearance, Singular in meaning

Look like plural (end in s) but take Singular verb.

Mathematics, Physics, News, Politics, Innings.

✔ Mathematics is a hard subject.

3. Singular in appearance, Plural in meaning

Look singular but take Plural verb.

Cattle, People, Police, Children.

✔ The cattle are grazing. (Not 'is', Not 'cattles')
