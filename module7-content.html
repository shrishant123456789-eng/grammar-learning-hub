1️⃣ Making Requests Politely

"Jab hum kisi se help maangte hain, sirf words nahi bolte — tone aur structure bhi matter karta hai. Order mat dijiye, request kijiye."

Magic Words: Could you / Can you / Would you

  • Formal (Polite): Could you please help me? (Best for strangers/elders)
  • Informal (Friends): Can you pass the water? (Okay for friends)
  • Very Polite: Would you mind helping me?

❌ Wrong vs ✅ Correct Sentences

❌ Rude / Direct✅ Polite / NaturalReason
Give me pen.Could you please give me a pen?Direct command vs Request.
Move aside.Excuse me, could you move a bit?Use 'Excuse me' to be soft.
I want water.Can I have some water, please?Don't state demand, ask for it.

2️⃣ Asking for Directions

Situation: You are lost in a new city. You need to find the metro station.

Key Phrases

  • "Excuse me, where is the nearest ATM?"
  • "How do I get to the railway station?"
  • "Is this the right way to the market?"

❌ Wrong vs ✅ Correct Sentences

❌ Wrong✅ Correct
Where hospital?Excuse me, where is the hospital?
Tell me way to park.Could you tell me the way to the park?

3️⃣ Giving Directions Clearly

"Jab koi rasta pooche, toh confusion mat create karein. Simple words use karein: Go straight, Turn left, Cross the road."

Vocabulary for Directions

  • Go straight: Seedhe jao.
  • Turn left/right: Baayein/Daayein mudo.
  • Go past the bank: Bank ke aage se niklo.
  • It's on your right: Ye aapke right side me hai.

4️⃣ Apologizing Correctly

Don't just say "Sorry". Make it meaningful.

Levels of Apology

  • Small Mistake: "Sorry about that." / "My bad." (Informal)
  • Medium Mistake: "I am sorry for the delay."
  • Big Mistake (Formal): "I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience."

❌ Wrong vs ✅ Correct Sentences

❌ Wrong✅ Correct
I do mistake.I made a mistake. I am sorry.
Sorry for cut phone.Sorry for hanging up.

5️⃣ Asking Permission

"Permission lene ke liye 'May I' sabse best hai formal situations me."

Sentence Patterns

  • May I come in? (Formal)
  • Do you mind if I sit here? (Very Polite)
  • Can I use your charger? (Friendly)

6️⃣ Offering Help

When you see someone struggling, use these phrases:

✅ "Can I help you?"
✅ "Do you need any help?"
✅ "Would you like me to carry this?"
✅ "Let me help you with that."

7️⃣ Making Suggestions

Instead of ordering "Do this", suggest "Let's do this".

Common Phrases

  • Let's: "Let's go for a movie."
  • How about: "How about we eat pizza tonight?"
  • Why don't we: "Why don't we start early?"

8️⃣ Common Functional Mistakes

❌ Mistake✅ CorrectionReason
Please reply me back.Please reply to me.'Back' is redundant with Reply.
I suggest you to go.I suggest that you go.Suggest follows 'that' clause usually.
Please explain me.Please explain it to me.Explain needs an object (it/this).

9️⃣ Speak Yourself (Role Play)

Stand in front of a mirror and act out these roles.

Scenario 1: At a Cafe
You want to order a coffee but with less sugar.
⬇️
Speak: "Excuse me, I would like to have a cold coffee. Could you please make it with less sugar?"
Scenario 2: In Office
You need a leave for tomorrow.
⬇️
Speak: "Sir, may I take a leave tomorrow? I have some urgent personal work."
