Gerund & Infinitive

Master the confusing world of V+ing vs To+V1

1. Introduction

What is the difference?
Both look like verbs, but they act like NOUNS.
• Gerund: Verb + ing (e.g., Swimming is fun.)
• Infinitive: To + Verb (e.g., I want to swim.)
Dono ka matlab hindi mein 'Na' se khatam hota hai (Tairna, Khana, Sona). Lekin English mein kab 'Ing' lagana hai aur kab 'To' lagana hai, iske rules hote hain.

Simple Examples:

  • Gerund: Reading books is my hobby. (Subject)
  • Infinitive: I like to read books. (Object)
  • Gerund: He started crying.
  • Infinitive: He decided to cry.
2️⃣ GERUND (Verb + ing as Noun)

Definition

A Gerund is a verb ending in -ing that functions as a Noun.

Jab koi Verb sentence mein Noun ka kaam kare (Subject ya Object ban jaye), use Gerund kehte hain.

Verbs followed by Gerund (Memorize This!)

If you use a verb after these words, it MUST be a Gerund:

AdmitAvoidConsiderDeny
EnjoyFinishKeepMind
MissPracticeQuitSuggest
Stop (cease)RecommendRiskPostpone

Examples:

  • I enjoy playing cricket. (Not 'to play')
  • He avoided talking to her.
  • Would you mind opening the window?
  • She suggested going for a movie.

Prepositions + Gerund

After any preposition (in, on, at, of, for, about, without), always use Gerund.

  • He is good at swimming.
  • Thank you for coming.
3️⃣ INFINITIVE (To + V1)

Definition

The Infinitive is the base form of a verb, usually preceded by 'to'. It shows purpose or intention.

Jab hum future ka plan, iccha (desire), ya purpose batate hain, tab Infinitive use hota hai.

Verbs followed by Infinitive

AgreeAppearChooseDecide
ExpectHopeLearnManage
OfferPlanPromiseRefuse
SeemWantWishWould like

Examples:

  • I want to go home.
  • She promised to help me.
  • We decided to buy a car.

Infinitive of Purpose

Use 'to' to answer 'Why?'.

  • I went to the shop to buy milk. (Why did I go?)
4️⃣ GERUND vs INFINITIVE (Tricky Cases)

Some verbs can take BOTH, but the meaning changes completely.

1. STOP

Stop + Gerund (V-ing) Stop + Infinitive (To V1)
Stop an activity forever/temporarily. Stop doing something else TO do this.
He stopped smoking.
(Usne cigarette peena chhod diya)		 He stopped to smoke.
(Wo cigarette peene ke liye ruka)

2. REMEMBER

Remember + Gerund Remember + Infinitive
Recall a past memory. Don't forget a task (Future).
I remember locking the door.
(Mujhe yaad hai maine lock kiya tha)		 Remember to lock the door.
(Lock karna yaad rakhna)

3. FORGET

  • Forget doing: Forget a past event. (I'll never forget meeting you.)
  • Forget to do: Forget a duty. (Don't forget to call me.)

4. TRY

  • Try doing: Experiment/Method (Try restarting your phone).
  • Try to do: Effort/Attempt (I tried to lift the box, but it was heavy).
