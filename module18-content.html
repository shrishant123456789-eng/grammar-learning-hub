The Science of "No Translation"

🇮🇳 Why Translation Kills Fluency?

Jab aap Hindi me sochte hain aur English me translate karte hain, toh aapka dimaag Double Processing karta hai. Isme time lagta hai aur fluency toot jaati hai.

Goal: Situation dekho -> Direct English Reaction nikle. Beech me Hindi nahi aani chahiye.

Example: Pair (foot) par chot lagne par "Ouch!" nikalna chahiye, "Arre lag gayi" nahi.

🇬🇧 Mastery Theory

  • Emotion First: Connect feelings directly to English words.
  • Micro-Responses: You don't need full sentences. "No way!" is a complete thought.
  • Speed: Train your brain to react in under 1 second.
